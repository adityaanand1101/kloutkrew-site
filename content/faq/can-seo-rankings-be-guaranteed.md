## What Google says

Google's guide to hiring an SEO is direct: no one can guarantee a #1 ranking, and if someone promises their changes will put you in first place, you should find someone else. You can read it in [Google's "Do you need an SEO?" guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo).

## Why guarantees are a red flag

- **Rankings aren't for sale.** Agencies can't control Google's systems.
- **"Guaranteed" keywords are often useless.** It's easy to rank for terms nobody searches.
- **Shortcuts carry risk.** Bought links and mass-produced pages can get a site demoted, which takes far longer to fix than it took to cause.

## What a good agency can promise

- A clear list of what will be done each month
- Technical fixes, content and outreach that follow Google's guidelines
- Honest reporting from Search Console and Analytics, including what isn't working
- Advice on what you need to do too, such as collecting reviews

## Questions to ask before hiring

- What exactly will you do in the first three months?
- How will you report progress, and from which tools?
- How do you build links, and can I see examples?
- What happens to the work and accounts if we stop?
