## What happens, roughly in order

1. **First weeks: fixes and indexing.** Fix technical problems, submit the sitemap, and make sure every important page can be found and read by Google and AI tools.
2. **First few months: early rankings.** Pages start appearing for specific, low-competition searches, such as a service plus a city or a detailed question.
3. **Months three to six and beyond: growth.** As content builds up and other sites start linking to you, rankings move up for broader, more competitive terms.

## What decides the speed

- **Competition.** "Logo design cost in India" is easier than "branding agency".
- **Your site's age and reputation.** New domains have no history, so trust takes time.
- **Content.** Pages that answer real questions in depth rank faster than thin pages.
- **Links and mentions.** Directories, reviews, press and guest articles tell search engines other people vouch for you.
- **Consistency.** Publishing steadily works better than a burst followed by silence.

## How to tell it's working before traffic arrives

Watch Google Search Console: impressions rising, more pages indexed and more search terms appearing usually come weeks before clicks do.
