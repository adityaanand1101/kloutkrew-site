## How remote projects work

Branding, packaging, websites and AI films are all made on screen, so location matters less than process. A remote project with KloutKrew runs like this:

- **Kickoff on a video call** to understand your business, customers and goals
- **Shared files** in Figma and a shared folder, so you can see work in progress
- **Written updates** at every milestone, so nothing depends on being on a call
- **Feedback** left as comments directly on the designs

## Time zones

We work on India Standard Time (UTC+5:30):

- **UAE and Saudi Arabia:** almost the full working day overlaps
- **UK and Europe:** your morning is our afternoon
- **US:** early morning or evening calls, with work progressing while you sleep

## Packaging that's printed abroad

We design to your printer's dieline and supply print-ready files, so packaging can be printed wherever you manufacture. You or your compliance advisor confirm the regulatory text for your market; we lay it out legibly.

## Websites for international brands

We build sites that load fast worldwide, support multiple languages and currencies where needed, and can be edited by your team. See our [website design service](/website-design-agency).

## What to ask any studio abroad

- Can I see work for brands in my market or category?
- Who will I speak to day to day, and when?
- What exactly do I receive at the end, and do I own it?
- How are payments split across the project?
