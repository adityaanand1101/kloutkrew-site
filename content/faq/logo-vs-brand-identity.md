## What a logo is

A logo is the mark people see most often: on your website header, your packaging, your app icon and your invoices. A good logo is simple, distinctive and works at every size, from a 16-pixel favicon to a shop sign. A logo project usually delivers a primary logo, a few lockups (stacked, horizontal) and an icon, in every file format.

## What a brand identity adds

A brand identity is everything that makes your brand recognisable even when the logo isn't on screen:

- **Colour palette** with exact values for screen and print
- **Typography**: which fonts to use for headlines and body text, and how
- **Imagery style**: photography, illustration or 3D, and how it's cropped and edited
- **Graphic elements**: patterns, shapes, icons and textures
- **Layouts and templates** for social posts, presentations and ads
- **Guidelines** so your team, printers and agencies apply it consistently

## Which one do you need?

A logo alone is enough when you're testing an idea, running a small local business or already have a design system you're happy with.

You need a full identity when you're launching a consumer brand, raising money, designing packaging, building a website or working with several freelancers and agencies. Without a system, every new asset is designed from scratch and the brand drifts.
