## The parts of a usage agreement

- **Channels:** organic social only, or also paid ads, your website, marketplaces, email and offline
- **Duration:** commonly a few months to a year for paid use, sometimes in perpetuity for a higher fee
- **Territory:** India only, specific countries or worldwide
- **Exclusivity:** whether the creator can work with competing brands during the term
- **Edits:** whether you can cut, re-edit, add captions or combine the footage with other content
- **Whitelisting:** whether ads can run from the creator's own handle, which needs separate permission

## Organic vs paid use

Paid use usually costs more than organic, because the video is pushed to many more people and the creator's face becomes part of your advertising. Agree paid rights upfront if there's any chance you'll run the video as an ad; adding them later is often slower and more expensive.

## What to put in writing

Before filming, a short agreement should list the deliverables, the fee, the rights above, the approval process and what happens when the term ends.
