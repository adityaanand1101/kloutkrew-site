## A typical timeline

1. **Week 1: brief and research.** A kickoff call about your business, customers and competitors, followed by a short written strategy: who the brand is for and how it should feel.
2. **Week 1–2: first concepts.** Two or three distinct directions, each shown on real applications such as a website header, a pack and a social post, so you can judge them in context.
3. **Week 2–4: refinement.** We develop the direction you choose: logo suite, colours, typography, imagery style and graphic elements. Usually two rounds of feedback.
4. **Week 4–6: rollout and handover.** Templates, brand guidelines and every file format, plus any first applications you've asked for (packaging, social templates, a pitch deck).

## What speeds a project up

- One decision-maker, or a small group that gives one combined round of feedback
- A clear brief: who you sell to, who you compete with, what you want to be known for
- Product details, copy and photos ready when we need them

## What slows it down

- New stakeholders joining halfway and reopening decisions
- Waiting on content such as product names or legal text
- Adding deliverables mid-project (a website or packaging range on top of the identity)

## Can it be faster?

For a launch date, we can compress the schedule by agreeing the scope upfront and booking feedback calls in advance. What we don't recommend is skipping research or concepts; that's where most of the value is.
