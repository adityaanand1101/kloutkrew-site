## What your team can usually change

- Text and images on existing pages
- Blog posts, case studies and news
- Products, prices and stock (on Shopify)
- Team members, job openings and testimonials
- Page titles and descriptions for search engines

## What usually needs a designer or developer

- New page layouts or sections that don't exist yet
- New features such as calculators, booking or member areas
- Changes to navigation and site structure
- Anything that affects speed, tracking or integrations

## How we set it up

We build editable sections and collections for anything you'll update often, so adding a blog post or product is filling in a form, not redesigning a page. At handover we walk your team through it and leave a short written guide.

## Which platform is easiest to edit?

- **Webflow:** visual editing for marketing sites with strong design
- **Shopify:** the simplest for products and orders
- **Framer:** fast for small launch sites
- **WordPress:** flexible, but quality depends heavily on the theme and plugins
