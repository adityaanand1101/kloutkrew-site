## What's included

- **Research:** your category, competitors on shelf and online, and what your buyers notice first
- **Concepts:** two or three design directions shown as realistic 3D mockups
- **Structure:** choosing or adapting the box, pouch, bottle or jar format with your manufacturer
- **Graphics and layout:** brand elements, product name, claims, imagery and the mandatory information
- **Range system:** colours and rules so every flavour, variant or size belongs to the same family
- **Print preparation:** artwork set on your printer's dieline, with bleed, finishes and colour modes checked
- **E-commerce assets:** listing images and mockups for Amazon, Flipkart and quick-commerce apps

## What a packaging agency doesn't do

An agency designs and prepares the files; your printer or converter manufactures the packs. Regulatory content, such as FSSAI details or ingredient lists, comes from you or your compliance advisor. The agency makes sure it's laid out legibly and to the space rules.

## Why it matters online

On marketplaces your pack is often a thumbnail a few centimetres wide. Bold, simple packs with one clear focal point do better there, so we test every design at thumbnail size.

## How to choose an agency

Look for packaging they've designed that went to print, not just mockups; ask how they work with printers; and check they can design a range, not only a single pack.
