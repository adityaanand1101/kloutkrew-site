## What full ownership should include

- **The rights to the final designs:** logo, identity, packaging artwork and website design
- **Source files:** editable Figma, Illustrator or design files, not only PNGs and PDFs
- **Every export:** SVG, PDF, EPS and PNG in colour, black and white
- **Accounts in your name:** domain, website platform, analytics and ad accounts

## What usually stays licensed

- **Fonts** are licensed by their foundries; you may need your own licence to use them in-house or on your website.
- **Stock photos, illustrations and music** come with their own licence terms.
- **Unused concepts** usually stay with the studio unless you agree otherwise.

## What to check in any contract

- When ownership transfers (often on final payment)
- Whether you get source files or only exports
- Whether the studio can show the work in its portfolio
- Who owns the website, domain and hosting accounts

## Trademarks are a separate step

Owning the design isn't the same as having a registered trademark. Before you invest heavily in a name and logo, have a trademark search done and consider registering it. We can prepare the files you'll need.
