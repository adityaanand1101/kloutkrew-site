## A typical website timeline

1. **Week 1: plan.** Goals, audience, pages and the platform (Webflow, Shopify, Framer or WordPress).
2. **Week 1–2: design.** Homepage and key page designs for desktop and mobile.
3. **Week 2–4: build.** Development, animations, forms, content management and integrations.
4. **Week 4–5: content and SEO.** Final copy and images, page titles, structured data and speed checks.
5. **Week 5–6: test and launch.** Testing on real phones and browsers, redirects from any old site, and training for your team.

## What makes it faster

- Copy and photos ready, or a clear plan for who's writing them
- An existing brand identity to design from
- One person who can approve designs

## What makes it slower

- Content that arrives after the design is approved
- Custom features such as booking systems, calculators or member areas
- Migrating a large old site with many pages and redirects

## Don't skip the launch checks

A site that looks finished can still be slow on mobile, miss its tracking or break old links. We check speed, SEO basics, forms and analytics before launch, so the site starts bringing in enquiries on day one.
