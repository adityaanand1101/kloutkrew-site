## Start from the learning phase

When an ad set launches, Meta spends a period testing who to show it to. Practitioners widely cite the same benchmark: an ad set leaves this learning phase after roughly 50 optimisation events in a week. Below that, results are unstable and hard to judge.

## A simple way to estimate

1. **Estimate your cost per result** for the event you'll optimise for (a purchase, a lead or an add-to-cart), from past campaigns or industry benchmarks.
2. **Multiply by 50** to get a weekly budget per ad set.
3. **If that's more than you can spend,** optimise for an earlier event that happens more often, such as add-to-cart instead of purchase, or run fewer ad sets so each gets enough budget.

For example, at ₹400 per lead, 50 leads a week is about ₹20,000 per ad set per week.

## Common mistakes with small budgets

- Splitting a small budget across many ad sets, so none get enough results
- Changing budgets or creatives every day, which restarts learning
- Judging results after two or three days
- Optimising for purchases when the budget can only buy a handful
