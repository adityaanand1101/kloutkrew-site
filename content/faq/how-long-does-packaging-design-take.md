## Stage by stage

1. **Brief and research (days 1–5):** category, competitors, product details and the mandatory information.
2. **Concepts (by day 7):** two or three directions as 3D mockups, including how they look as a marketplace thumbnail.
3. **Refinement (weeks 2–3):** develop the chosen direction on the hero SKU, then roll it out across the range.
4. **Artwork (weeks 3–5):** final artwork on your printer's dieline, copy checks and proofs.
5. **Handover (weeks 4–6):** print-ready files, listing images and mockups.

## What adds time

- **New structures.** A custom box or bottle shape needs sampling with your manufacturer.
- **Many variants.** Each flavour or size needs its own artwork and checks.
- **Late content.** Ingredient lists, claims or legal text that arrive late delay final artwork.
- **Print proofs.** Physical proofs or press checks add days, but catch colour problems before a full run.

## How to keep to your launch date

- Confirm your printer and their dieline before artwork starts
- Collect all mandatory text early and have it checked by whoever signs off compliance
- Order a physical proof for the first run, especially for special finishes like foil or embossing
