KloutKrew Studio is a **packaging design agency** based in New Delhi. We design labels, boxes, pouches, bottles and mailer boxes for D2C, food, beverage, personal care and beauty brands across India, and for brands abroad that print in India or elsewhere.

## What we design

- **Labels and stickers** for jars, bottles and cans.
- **Boxes and cartons**, including mono cartons, rigid boxes and e-commerce mailers.
- **Pouches and sachets** for snacks, supplements and personal care.
- **Range architecture**, so variants and flavours are easy to tell apart while still looking like one family.
- **Mockups and listing images** for your website, Amazon, Flipkart and quick-commerce apps.

## What makes packaging sell

Most packs are judged in two places now: a crowded shelf and a phone screen a few centimetres wide. We design for both. That means one clear focal point, a brand mark that reads at thumbnail size, a colour that owns the category, and the key claim placed where a buyer's eye lands first.

## How a packaging project runs

1. **Brief and shelf audit.** We look at your category, competitors and where your product will actually be sold.
2. **First concepts in 7 days.** Two or three directions shown on 3D mockups and at thumbnail size.
3. **Refine and extend.** We develop the chosen route across every size and variant.
4. **Print-ready handover.** Files built to your printer's dielines, with finishes marked, plus mockups for marketing.

## Designed for the shelf and the screen

Most Indian D2C products are now sold in two places at once: on a physical shelf and as a thumbnail on Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Zepto or Instamart. A pack that works on a shelf at arm's length can disappear at 150 pixels wide. We design for both:

- **One clear focal point.** Usually the brand or the product name, that still reads at thumbnail size
- **Strong colour blocking** so each variant is easy to tell apart in a grid of results
- **A consistent layout** across the range, so the brand is recognisable before the name is read
- **Listing images** designed alongside the pack: hero shots, benefit callouts, ingredient and size images

See our guides to [packaging for quick commerce](/blog/packaging-for-quick-commerce) and [Amazon listing images](/blog/amazon-listing-images-guide).

## Mandatory information, laid out properly

Indian food, cosmetic and consumer packaging carries a lot of required text: product name, ingredients, net quantity, MRP, manufacturer details, batch and dates, FSSAI licence and veg or non-veg marks for food, and customer care details. We plan the layout around these from the start, so they're legible and meet the space rules without crowding the design. You or your compliance advisor confirm the content itself; we make sure it fits and reads well.

## Working with your printer

We ask for your printer's dieline before artwork starts and design directly onto it. Before handover we check bleed, safe zones, colour mode, special finishes such as foil, spot UV or embossing, and barcode placement. For a first print run we recommend a physical proof; screens and paper never match exactly. If you don't have a printer yet, we'll tell you what to ask for when you get quotes.

## Range planning

Launching with three variants but planning ten? We design the system for ten. A good range system sets rules for what changes between variants (colour, illustration, flavour name) and what never does (logo position, layout grid, typography), so adding new SKUs later is quick and consistent.

## What affects the price

The number of SKUs, whether a new structure (a custom box or bottle shape) is needed, how much illustration or photography is involved, and how many rounds of print checks are required.
