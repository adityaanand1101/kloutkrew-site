KloutKrew Studio is a **packaging design agency** based in New Delhi. We design labels, boxes, pouches, bottles and mailer boxes for D2C, food, beverage, personal care and beauty brands across India, and for brands abroad that print in India or elsewhere.

## What we design

- **Labels and stickers** for jars, bottles and cans.
- **Boxes and cartons**, including mono cartons, rigid boxes and e-commerce mailers.
- **Pouches and sachets** for snacks, supplements and personal care.
- **Range architecture**, so variants and flavours are easy to tell apart while still looking like one family.
- **Mockups and listing images** for your website, Amazon, Flipkart and quick-commerce apps.

## What makes packaging sell

Most packs are judged in two places now: a crowded shelf and a phone screen a few centimetres wide. We design for both. That means one clear focal point, a brand mark that reads at thumbnail size, a colour that owns the category, and the key claim placed where a buyer's eye lands first.

## How a packaging project runs

1. **Brief and shelf audit.** We look at your category, competitors and where your product will actually be sold.
2. **First concepts in 7 days.** Two or three directions shown on 3D mockups and at thumbnail size.
3. **Refine and extend.** We develop the chosen route across every size and variant.
4. **Print-ready handover.** Files built to your printer's dielines, with finishes marked, plus mockups for marketing.
