KloutKrew Studio is an independent creative studio based in New Delhi. We design **brand identities and logos** for startups and D2C brands, in India and around the world. A good brand identity makes a young company look established, helps customers remember it and makes every later piece of design faster to make.

## What's included

- **Brand strategy.** Who you're for, what you stand for and how you're different from the brands you compete with.
- **Logo design.** A primary logo, a simplified mark for small sizes and social avatars, and lockups for every layout.
- **Colour and type.** A palette and typefaces chosen to work on screen, in print and on packaging.
- **Visual language.** Imagery style, patterns, icons and layout rules.
- **Brand guidelines.** A clear guide your team, printers and freelancers can follow.
- **Launch assets.** Social templates, business cards, a pitch deck cover or whatever you need on day one.

## How we work

1. **Kickoff and research.** A call to understand your business, customers and competitors, plus a look at what's working in your category.
2. **First concepts in 7 days.** Two or three distinct directions, each shown on real applications so you can judge them properly.
3. **Refine.** We develop the direction you choose into a full system.
4. **Hand over.** Final files, guidelines and launch assets, usually 4 to 6 weeks after kickoff.

## Who it's for

Founders launching a new brand, D2C brands moving from marketplace listings to their own website, and growing companies whose old logo no longer fits. We work with clients across India and remotely with brands in the US, UK, UAE, Australia and Europe.
