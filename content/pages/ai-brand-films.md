KloutKrew Studio makes **brand films, ad films and product videos** using AI production alongside traditional direction, editing and colour. You get cinematic launch films and campaign ads, sized for every channel, without the cost and time of a full shoot.

## What we make

- **Launch and brand films** that introduce a new brand or product.
- **Ad films** for Meta, YouTube and connected TV, with cut-downs for every placement.
- **Product videos** that show a product in settings that would be expensive to shoot.
- **Social content**, including Reels, Shorts and looping visuals for your feed.
- **Remasters** that upscale and regrade existing footage.

## Where AI helps, and where it doesn't

AI production is fastest and best value for imagined worlds, product visualisation, mood and motion, and for testing several creative directions quickly. It still needs a human eye for story, pacing, sound and brand consistency, so every film is storyboarded, directed and finished by our team. When a brief needs real people with your real product in hand, we'll recommend a shoot or a hybrid.

## How a film project runs

1. **Brief and script.** The message, audience and where the film will run.
2. **Storyboard.** Frames you approve before production starts.
3. **Production.** AI generation, any footage, editing, sound and colour.
4. **Delivery.** Master film and channel cut-downs, usually 2 to 4 weeks after the brief.

## What an AI brand film is

An AI brand film is a short video for your brand where some or all of the visuals are generated with AI tools, then directed, edited, scored and finished by a creative team. The result looks like a produced film, but without a full shoot: no location booking, large crew or set build. It works well for launch films, concept films, product visualisation, social ads and pitch videos.

## A typical film, stage by stage

1. **Brief and concept.** What the film must do (launch, explain, sell), where it will run and how long it should be.
2. **Script and storyboard.** Every shot planned before generation starts, so the film has a story rather than a string of pretty clips.
3. **Look development.** A visual style frame: lighting, colour, camera feel and how your product or brand appears.
4. **Generation and direction.** Shots generated, selected and refined; this is where most of the craft goes.
5. **Edit, sound and finishing.** Editing, music, voiceover, titles, colour and formats for each platform.

## Getting your product right

AI tools can drift: logos warp, labels change, hands look odd. For product shots we combine real product photography with generated environments, or composite your actual packaging into the scene, so the product on screen matches what customers receive. Close-ups of real people using the product are often better shot traditionally; we'll tell you which approach suits each shot.

## Rights and disclosure

We use tools whose terms allow commercial use, avoid recognisable real people and protected characters, and keep records of how each shot was made. Some platforms and markets expect AI-generated content to be labelled; we'll advise on what applies where your film will run.

## Formats you get

A master film plus cutdowns: 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 1:1 or 4:5 for feeds, 16:9 for YouTube and your website, and short versions for ads.
