KloutKrew Studio makes **brand films, ad films and product videos** using AI production alongside traditional direction, editing and colour. You get cinematic launch films and campaign ads, sized for every channel, without the cost and time of a full shoot.

## What we make

- **Launch and brand films** that introduce a new brand or product.
- **Ad films** for Meta, YouTube and connected TV, with cut-downs for every placement.
- **Product videos** that show a product in settings that would be expensive to shoot.
- **Social content**, including Reels, Shorts and looping visuals for your feed.
- **Remasters** that upscale and regrade existing footage.

## Where AI helps, and where it doesn't

AI production is fastest and best value for imagined worlds, product visualisation, mood and motion, and for testing several creative directions quickly. It still needs a human eye for story, pacing, sound and brand consistency, so every film is storyboarded, directed and finished by our team. When a brief needs real people with your real product in hand, we'll recommend a shoot or a hybrid.

## How a film project runs

1. **Brief and script.** The message, audience and where the film will run.
2. **Storyboard.** Frames you approve before production starts.
3. **Production.** AI generation, any footage, editing, sound and colour.
4. **Delivery.** Master film and channel cut-downs, usually 2 to 4 weeks after the brief.
