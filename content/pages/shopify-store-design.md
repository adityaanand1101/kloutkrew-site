Your store is where browsing becomes buying. KloutKrew Studio designs and builds **Shopify stores that look like your brand and make purchasing easy on a phone**.

## What we build

- **Homepage** with a clear offer and best-sellers
- **Collection pages** with useful filters
- **Product pages** with strong photos, benefits, reviews and sticky add-to-cart
- **Brand pages:** about, ingredients or materials, FAQs
- **Apps and integrations:** reviews, subscriptions, WhatsApp, email
- **Tracking:** GA4, Meta and Google conversion events

## Designed for mobile

Most D2C traffic is on phones. We design and test mobile first. See [Shopify store design for D2C brands](/blog/shopify-store-design-d2c).

## How it runs

1. Brief and store structure
2. Design of key pages, with first concepts within 7 days
3. Build and product setup
4. Testing on real phones
5. Launch, usually 4 to 6 weeks after kickoff
