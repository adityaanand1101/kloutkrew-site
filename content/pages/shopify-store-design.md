Your store is where browsing becomes buying. KloutKrew Studio designs and builds **Shopify stores that look like your brand and make purchasing easy on a phone**.

## What we build

- **Homepage** with a clear offer and best-sellers
- **Collection pages** with useful filters
- **Product pages** with strong photos, benefits, reviews and sticky add-to-cart
- **Brand pages:** about, ingredients or materials, FAQs
- **Apps and integrations:** reviews, subscriptions, WhatsApp, email
- **Tracking:** GA4, Meta and Google conversion events

## Designed for mobile

Most D2C traffic is on phones, and in our [2026 speed study of 53 Indian D2C homepages](/blog/indian-d2c-website-speed-study-2026) none scored "good" on mobile. We design and test mobile first, with a speed budget for every app and image. See [Shopify store design for D2C brands](/blog/shopify-store-design-d2c).

## What's included in a Shopify build

- **Store setup:** theme, payments, shipping zones, taxes, domains and email notifications
- **Custom design** of the homepage, collection pages, product pages, cart and key landing pages
- **Product page structure** that sells: benefit-led copy, image galleries, reviews, FAQs, size or variant guides and clear delivery information
- **Apps chosen carefully:** reviews, subscriptions, WhatsApp, upsells, loyalty, only where they earn their place
- **Indian checkout essentials:** UPI, COD options, pincode-based delivery estimates and GST-ready invoices, set up with the providers you choose
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics, Meta and Google Ads tracking, configured to report real orders

## Speed is a design decision

Every app you install adds code to every page. In our [study of 53 Indian D2C homepages](/blog/indian-d2c-website-speed-study-2026), the median page downloaded 2.6 MB of JavaScript, much of it from apps and trackers. We set a speed budget at the start, compress and size images for phones, load chat and review widgets only when needed, and test on a mid-range Android phone before launch.

## Product pages that convert

The product page is where the sale is won or lost. We design it to answer, in order: what is it, why is it better, what do other buyers say, what does it cost with delivery, and when will it arrive. Good product photography and short videos make the biggest difference; we can produce them with our [content team](/content-ugc-production).

## Migrating to Shopify

Moving from WooCommerce, Wix or a custom site? We migrate products, customers and order history where possible, and set up redirects from every old URL so you keep your search rankings. See [Shopify vs WooCommerce](/blog/shopify-vs-woocommerce) if you're still deciding.

## After launch

You get training for your team, every login and a simple guide to adding products and running sales. We also offer monthly support for new landing pages, seasonal campaigns and app reviews. Read our guide to [Shopify store design for D2C brands](/blog/shopify-store-design-d2c).
