Your logo is the most repeated piece of your brand. KloutKrew Studio designs **original, simple and memorable logos** that work everywhere from a 16-pixel favicon to a shop sign.

## What's included

- **Short brief and research** into your business, customers and competitors
- **Two or three concepts** shown on real applications
- **Refinement** of your chosen direction
- **Full logo suite:** primary, secondary lockups and icon
- **Every file format** for screens and print

## Logo or full brand identity?

A logo is one mark. If you also need colours, typography, packaging, a website and templates, choose [brand identity design](/branding-agency) so everything is designed together.

Simplicity, a distinctive silhouette, one clear idea and careful proportions, rather than trends that date quickly. Read [timeless logo design](/blog/timeless-logo-design).

## What makes a good logo

- **Simple.** It can be recognised at a glance and drawn from memory. Detail that disappears at small sizes doesn't belong in the main mark.
- **Distinctive.** It doesn't borrow the shapes and colours every competitor uses. In crowded categories such as skincare, coffee or SaaS, standing apart matters more than looking "right" for the category.
- **Works in one colour.** A logo that only works in a gradient fails on rubber stamps, embroidery, foil and newspaper ads.
- **Scalable.** It stays crisp from a 16-pixel browser tab to signage several metres wide.
- **Appropriate.** It suits your price point and audience. A luxury brand and a budget snack brand need different personalities.

## Types of logos we design

- **Wordmarks**, where the name itself is the logo, set in custom-drawn or customised lettering. Best when the name is short and distinctive.
- **Lettermarks and monograms**, built from initials, useful for long names and app icons.
- **Symbols and icons**, abstract or pictorial marks that can stand alone once the brand is known.
- **Combination marks**, a symbol plus wordmark, with lockups for horizontal and stacked layouts.
- **Emblems and badges**, where the name sits inside a shape, often used for food, beverage and heritage brands.

Most of our clients end up with a combination mark plus a simplified icon, because that covers every use from packaging to social avatars.

## What you'll receive

- Primary, secondary and icon versions
- Colour, black and white (reversed) versions
- SVG, PDF and EPS vector files for print and signage
- PNG files at several sizes, plus favicon and social avatar sizes
- A one-page usage sheet: clear space, minimum sizes, colours and what not to do

## Avoid these common mistakes

- **Choosing by committee.** Collect everyone's input at the brief stage, then let one person decide.
- **Judging a logo on a white screen.** Always look at it on real applications: your packaging, website header and a phone home screen. That's why we present concepts that way.
- **Skipping the trademark check.** Before you print anything, have a trademark search done in the classes you trade in.
- **Over-explaining.** A logo doesn't have to tell your whole story. Recognition comes from consistent use over time.
