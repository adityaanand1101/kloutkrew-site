Your logo is the most repeated piece of your brand. KloutKrew Studio designs **original, simple and memorable logos** that work everywhere from a 16-pixel favicon to a shop sign.

## What's included

- **Short brief and research** into your business, customers and competitors
- **Two or three concepts** shown on real applications
- **Refinement** of your chosen direction
- **Full logo suite:** primary, secondary lockups and icon
- **Every file format** for screens and print
- **Simple usage rules** so others use it correctly

## Logo or full brand identity?

A logo is one mark. If you also need colours, typography, packaging, a website and templates, choose [brand identity design](/branding-agency) so everything is designed together.

## Our principles

Simplicity, a distinctive silhouette, one clear idea and careful proportions, rather than trends that date quickly. Read [timeless logo design](/blog/timeless-logo-design).
