KloutKrew Studio is an independent creative studio based in New Delhi, working with **startups and D2C brands around the world**. We design brand identities, packaging, websites and AI brand films, all made by one team, so everything looks like one brand.

## What we make

- **Brand identity and logo design.** Strategy, logo, colours, type and a brand guide your team can use.
- **Packaging design.** Labels, boxes and pouches delivered print-ready for your printer, wherever it is.
- **Websites.** Fast Webflow and Shopify sites built to turn visitors into enquiries and sales.
- **Social media creatives.** Launch campaigns and monthly content in your brand's look.
- **AI brand films.** Launch and product films made with AI, without the cost of a full shoot.

## How remote projects work

1. **Kickoff call.** A video call to learn about your brand, customers and competitors. We reply to every enquiry within 24 hours.
2. **First concepts in 7 days.** Two or three directions to react to, shared as files you can comment on directly.
3. **Refine and build.** Written updates at every milestone, so you always know where the project stands.
4. **Launch.** Most projects are live in 4 to 6 weeks, with every source file handed over.

## Why brands work with us from abroad

You get one design team that handles brand, packaging and website together, instead of juggling three freelancers. Our time zone overlaps with yours for calls, and work keeps moving while you're offline.
