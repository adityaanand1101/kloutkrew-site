KloutKrew Studio is an independent creative studio in **New Delhi**. We help startups and D2C brands across **Delhi, Gurugram, Noida and the rest of NCR** look sharper and sell better, with branding, packaging, websites and films made by one team.

## What we do for Delhi NCR brands

- **Brand identity and logo design.** Name, logo, colours, type and a brand guide your team can actually use.
- **Packaging design.** Labels, boxes and pouches for D2C, food, beverage and beauty brands, delivered print-ready.
- **Websites.** Fast Webflow and Shopify sites, designed to turn visitors into enquiries and orders.
- **Social media creatives.** Launch campaigns and monthly content in your brand's look.
- **AI brand films.** Product and launch films made with AI, without the cost of a full shoot.

## Why brands pick a local studio

Working with a studio in your own city means you can meet in person for the kickoff, see printed packaging samples together and get feedback rounds turned around quickly. You still get the same process we use for clients abroad: clear milestones, one point of contact, and every file handed over at the end.

## How a project runs

1. **Kickoff call.** We learn about your brand, customers and competitors. We reply to every enquiry within 24 hours.
2. **First concepts in 7 days.** You get two or three directions to react to, not a single take-it-or-leave-it option.
3. **Refine and build.** We develop the chosen direction across logo, packaging, website or social.
4. **Launch.** Most projects are live in 4 to 6 weeks, with every source file handed over.
