KloutKrew Studio designs and builds **websites that look like your brand and turn visitors into enquiries and sales**. We build on Webflow, Framer, WordPress and Shopify, and design interfaces for apps and SaaS products, for clients in India and worldwide.

## What we build

- **Marketing websites** for startups and service businesses, usually on Webflow or Framer.
- **Online stores** on Shopify for D2C brands, from product pages to checkout.
- **Portfolio sites** for architects, interior designers and creative studios.
- **Real estate project sites** with floor plans, galleries and enquiry forms.
- **UI/UX design** for apps, SaaS dashboards and design systems.

## Built to be found and to load fast

Every site we build ships with clean page titles and descriptions, structured data for Google, compressed images and video, and a sitemap. We check speed with Google Lighthouse before launch, because slow pages lose visitors and rankings.

## How a website project runs

1. **Brief.** We gather goals, audiences, features and content.
2. **Wireframes.** Simple layouts that settle structure and page flow before any visual design.
3. **UI design.** Polished page designs in your brand, with the first concepts within 7 days.
4. **Build and launch.** Development, content setup, testing on phones and browsers, then launch, usually 4 to 6 weeks after kickoff.

## Which platform we'll recommend

- **Webflow** for marketing sites, portfolios and service businesses that want strong design and easy editing without plugins.
- **Shopify** for selling products online, with checkout, payments, inventory and shipping handled.
- **Framer** for fast, simple launch sites and landing pages.
- **WordPress** when you need a specific plugin, a very large blog or an existing WordPress team.

We'll recommend one in the first call based on what you sell and who will update the site. See [Webflow vs Framer vs WordPress](/blog/webflow-vs-framer-vs-wordpress) for the trade-offs.

## What a good business website does

- **Answers the visitor's first three questions in the first screen:** what you do, who it's for, and what to do next.
- **Loads fast on a phone.** Most visitors arrive on mobile, often from Instagram or a search on a slow connection. Our own [speed study of 53 Indian D2C homepages](/blog/indian-d2c-website-speed-study-2026) found the median took about 7 seconds to show its main content.
- **Shows proof:** work, results, client names, reviews and real photos, not stock images.
- **Makes it easy to get in touch:** a short form, WhatsApp and phone, visible on every page.
- **Is easy for your team to update.** So it doesn't go stale six months after launch.

## SEO and speed built in

Every site we build ships with clean page titles and descriptions, structured data for Google, a sitemap, fast image formats, and Core Web Vitals checks before launch. We also set up Google Analytics and Search Console so you can see what's working from day one. If you're replacing an old site, we map every old address to its new page so you don't lose the rankings you have.
