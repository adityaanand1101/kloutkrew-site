Ads work when strong creative meets disciplined testing. KloutKrew Studio runs **Meta Ads and Google Ads** with creatives made by our own design and video team.

## What we do

- **Strategy:** audiences, offers, budgets and goals
- **Creatives:** static, video, UGC and carousel ads. See [Meta ads creative testing](/blog/meta-ads-creative-testing)
- **Campaign setup and management** on Meta and Google
- **Landing pages** built to convert. See [landing page design](/blog/landing-page-design-that-converts)
- **Tracking:** GA4 and platform conversion events
- **Reporting:** results that matter, such as cost per lead or sale

## Channels we run

- **Meta ads** (Instagram and Facebook) for most consumer brands: prospecting, retargeting and catalogue ads
- **Google ads:** search for high-intent demand, Performance Max and Shopping for e-commerce, YouTube for video
- **Marketplace ads** on Amazon and Flipkart, where your buyers already search

## Tracking comes first

Ads optimise towards what you measure, so we set up tracking before spending: the Meta pixel and Conversions API, Google Ads conversions, and Google Analytics, all tested with real test orders or leads. Without this, platforms optimise for clicks instead of customers.

## Creative is the biggest lever

On Meta especially, the ad creative now does most of the targeting. We plan a steady flow of new creatives, test several hooks and formats, and scale what works. Because our team also produces [content and UGC](/content-ugc-production), new ads don't wait on another agency. Read our guide to [Meta ads creative testing](/blog/meta-ads-creative-testing).

## Budgets and the learning phase

Meta's delivery system needs enough results each week to stabilise, so a starting budget has to be big enough for your cost per result. We'll suggest a realistic test budget and structure before you spend. See [what budget you need when starting](/faq/how-much-to-spend-on-ads-when-starting).

## Reporting you can act on

A weekly check-in and a monthly report covering spend, cost per lead or order, return on ad spend, what we tested and what's next. Campaigns run in your own ad accounts, so you always own the data and access.
