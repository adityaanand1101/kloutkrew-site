KloutKrew Studio designs **apps, SaaS products and websites that people understand and enjoy using**. We combine UX thinking with strong visual design, so your product is easy to use and looks like your brand.

## What we design

- **Mobile apps** for iOS and Android
- **SaaS dashboards** and web apps
- **Marketing websites** and landing pages
- **Onboarding flows** that get users to their first win. See [app onboarding UX](/blog/app-onboarding-ux)
- **Design systems** that keep every screen consistent. See [design systems for startups](/blog/design-system-for-startups)

## How a UI/UX project runs

1. **Discovery.** Goals, users, competitors and analytics.
2. **UX.** User flows, information architecture and wireframes.
3. **UI.** Polished screens in your brand, with first directions within 7 days.
4. **Prototype and test.** Clickable flows tested with real users.
5. **Handover.** Design system, specs and assets for developers.

## Why it matters

Small usability problems quietly cost sign-ups, sales and support time. Good UX makes the important actions obvious, and good UI builds trust at first glance.

Read [wireframe vs mockup vs prototype](/blog/wireframe-vs-mockup-vs-prototype), or [tell us about your product](/contact-us).

## What a UI/UX project includes

- **Research:** stakeholder interviews, a review of your analytics and support tickets, and, where possible, conversations with real users.
- **User flows:** the steps a user takes to complete each key task, from sign-up to checkout or booking.
- **Wireframes:** low-detail layouts to agree structure and content before visual design.
- **Clickable prototypes** in Figma that you can test with users and show investors.
- **Visual design:** final screens for mobile and desktop, in light and dark mode where needed.
- **A design system:** reusable components, colours, type and spacing rules so your product stays consistent as it grows.
- **Developer handover:** annotated files, specs and assets, plus time to answer your developers' questions.

## Redesign or improve?

A full redesign isn't always the answer. If people already use your product, we often start with an audit: where do users drop off, which screens cause support tickets, which tasks take too many steps? Fixing the three biggest problems usually beats redesigning everything. See [usability testing on a budget](/blog/usability-testing-on-a-budget) for how to find them.

## Common UX problems we fix

- Sign-up forms that ask for too much, too early
- Navigation built around your org chart instead of user tasks
- Checkout flows with surprise costs or forced account creation
- Dashboards that show every metric and highlight none
- Inconsistent buttons, spacing and patterns that make the product feel unfinished
- Poor contrast and tiny tap targets that fail accessibility basics

## What we design for

SaaS dashboards, mobile apps, marketplaces, booking flows, internal tools and e-commerce. We work with your in-house developers or partner agencies, and we're comfortable designing within an existing design system or building one from scratch.

## Deliverables and timelines

A focused audit and fix of a few key flows can take a few weeks. A new product from research to a full design system takes longer and is usually split into phases, so developers can start building while later screens are designed.
