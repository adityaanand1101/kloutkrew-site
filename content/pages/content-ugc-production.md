Brands that post consistently, with content people actually want to watch, stay top of mind. KloutKrew Studio plans and produces **Reels, UGC videos, product photography and ad creatives** in batches, so you always have fresh content ready.

## What we produce

- **Reels and short-form videos** with strong hooks. See [reel hooks](/blog/reel-hooks)
- **UGC creator videos** for ads and social proof
- **Product photography** and AI-assisted lifestyle imagery
- **Ad creatives** in every format, built for testing
- **Behind-the-scenes and founder content**

## How it works

1. **Plan.** Content pillars and a monthly calendar. See [content calendar guide](/blog/content-calendar-guide)
2. **Brief.** Hooks, messages and shot lists. See [UGC brief template](/blog/ugc-brief-template)
3. **Produce.** Creators, shoots and AI-assisted visuals
4. **Edit and deliver.** Captions, cut-downs and formats for every platform
5. **Learn.** Keep what performs, drop what doesn't

## Why one team helps

The same people plan, produce and edit, so every video stays on-brand and lessons from one batch improve the next. Pair it with [performance marketing](/performance-marketing) to put the best content behind ads.
