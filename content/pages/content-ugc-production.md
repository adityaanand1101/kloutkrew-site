## Types of content we produce

- **UGC videos:** creators showing your product in use, in the style people already watch on Reels and Shorts
- **Founder and team videos:** behind-the-scenes, how it's made, why you started
- **Product demos and unboxings** for listings and ads
- **Static creatives:** product shots, benefit cards and carousel posts
- **Ad variations:** several hooks and edits of the same video so you can test what works

## How we find and brief creators

We match creators to your audience: age, city, language and the kind of content they already make. Each creator gets a written brief with the hook options, key messages, must-show moments and things to avoid, so the content feels natural but still sells. See our [UGC brief template](/blog/ugc-brief-template).

## Usage rights, agreed upfront

Before anyone films, we agree where and how long each video can be used: organic social, paid ads, your website or marketplaces. That avoids the common problem of finding a great video and discovering you can't run it as an ad. Read [who owns UGC content](/faq/who-owns-ugc-content).

## What makes UGC work

- **The first two seconds:** a clear hook, such as a problem, a surprising result or a question
- **Real use, not reading a script:** the product doing its job on screen
- **One message per video.** Not a list of every feature
- **Volume and testing:** several creators and hooks, then scaling the winners

## A monthly rhythm

Most brands work with us monthly: a planning call, a batch of creator videos and static creatives, delivery in platform-ready formats, then a look at what performed so the next batch is better.
