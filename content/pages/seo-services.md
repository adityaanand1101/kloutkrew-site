## Technical SEO

We fix what stops search engines from finding and understanding your pages: crawl errors, redirects, duplicate pages, slow loading, missing structured data, broken internal links and sitemap problems. For new sites, we set this up right from day one.

## Content that answers real searches

We research what your customers actually search for, from "branding agency in Delhi" to detailed questions like "how much does packaging design cost", then plan pages and articles that answer them better than what's ranking now. Thin, generic pages don't rank; useful, specific ones do.

## Local SEO

For businesses that serve a city, we set up and maintain your Google Business Profile, keep your name, address and phone consistent across directories, and plan a review strategy. See our [Google Business Profile guide](/blog/google-business-profile-guide) and [local SEO for Delhi businesses](/blog/local-seo-delhi).

## AI search optimisation

ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews and Perplexity recommend businesses they can describe confidently. That depends on clear facts on your site, structured data, answer-style content, and mentions on other trusted sites such as directories, reviews and press. We build all of these. Read [how to get your brand recommended by ChatGPT](/blog/ai-search-visibility-geo).

## Links and mentions

Rankings depend heavily on other sites linking to you. We earn links through directories, original research, guest articles and PR, never by buying them, because bought links put your site at risk.

## Honest expectations

SEO takes months, not weeks, and nobody can guarantee rankings. See [how long SEO takes](/faq/how-long-does-seo-take) and [why guarantees are a red flag](/faq/can-seo-rankings-be-guaranteed). We report monthly from Search Console and Analytics, including what isn't working yet.
