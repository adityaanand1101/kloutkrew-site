## How to read the numbers

- **One-time** items are design and build fees. Printing, Shopify subscription, apps and hosting are paid separately.
- **Monthly** items are agency or freelancer fees, multiplied by the number of months you choose. Ad spend is added on its own line.
- **Open-ended** ranges (marked with a +) mean published guides give a starting point but no ceiling.
