Most brands need a steady stream of design: a new deck, a festival campaign, a brochure for an event, a week of social posts. KloutKrew Studio handles **graphic design that stays on-brand**, whatever the format.

| Digital | Social creatives, banners, email graphics |
| Presentations | Pitch decks, sales decks, reports |

| Campaigns | Launch and festive campaign assets |

Every piece follows your colours, typography and tone, so a poster, a deck and an Instagram post all look like the same company. See [brand consistency across channels](/blog/brand-consistency-across-channels).

## Common requests

- **Pitch decks and sales presentations** that look as good as your product
- **Social media creatives:** post templates, carousels and story designs your team can reuse
- **Marketing collateral:** brochures, catalogues, flyers, menus and posters
- **Event and exhibition graphics:** stalls, standees, banners and badges
- **Packaging inserts and labels.** Thank-you cards and stickers
- **Infographics and report design** that make data easy to read

## Templates your team can use

Much of the value is in templates: a set of Canva or Figma layouts your team can edit without breaking the brand. We design them with clear locked and editable areas, and include a short guide so new team members get it right.

## Print-ready, every time

For print, we set up files with the right size, bleed, colour mode and resolution, check fonts and images, and can coordinate with your printer. For screens, we export the right formats and sizes for each platform.

## Turnaround

Small requests like a social post set or a flyer are usually turned around within a few working days; larger pieces such as a pitch deck or brochure are planned with you. For ongoing work, a monthly retainer gives you a set amount of design time and priority scheduling.

## When you need more than graphics

If your brand itself feels inconsistent, graphics alone won't fix it. Start with a [brand identity](/branding-agency), then design everything from the same system. See [graphics design trends for 2026](/blog/graphics-design-trends-2026).
