KloutKrew Studio plans, makes and distributes **content that sells**. We design social posts and Reels in your brand's look, produce UGC and ad creatives, and run Meta Ads, Google Ads and SEO so the content reaches the right people.

## What we do

- **Social media management.** A content calendar, designed posts and Reels, captions and scheduling.
- **Content and UGC.** Reels, shorts, series and creator content made for each platform.
- **Performance ads.** Ad creatives built for testing, from concept to final cut, for Meta and Google.
- **Paid campaigns.** Setup, targeting, testing and budget management.
- **SEO.** On-page and technical SEO, content plans and local SEO.

## Why one team for content and ads

When the people making the creative also see the ad results, the next round of content gets better every week. You don't wait for an agency hand-off, and every post and ad still looks like your brand.

## How it runs

1. **Audit and plan.** We review your channels, competitors and goals, then agree a monthly plan.
2. **Produce.** Posts, Reels and ad creatives made in batches, approved by you before anything goes live.
3. **Publish and promote.** Scheduling, community replies if needed, and paid campaigns.
4. **Report and improve.** A monthly report on reach, engagement and sales, with the next month's plan.
