This policy explains what information KloutKrew Studio ("we", "us") collects through kloutkrew.in, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and in plain language.

## Who we are

KloutKrew Studio is a branding, design and marketing studio in New Delhi, India. For anything about your data, contact us at [connect@kloutkrew.in](mailto:connect@kloutkrew.in) or +91 83750 35325.

## What we collect

- **What you send us.** When you use a form on this site (contact, free website review, newsletter), we receive what you enter: for example your name, email, phone number, website, budget and message.
- **Where an enquiry came from.** With a form submission we include the page you sent it from and, if available, the first page you visited and the website or campaign that brought you here, so we know which content helps people find us.
- **Usage data.** We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: pages viewed, buttons clicked, how far people scroll, device and browser type, and approximate location (city or country). It doesn't tell us who you are.
- **Messages you start yourself.** If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we receive whatever you send through those services.

## Cookies and similar storage

When you first visit, we ask which kinds of cookies you're happy with. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie settings** in the footer.

- **Essential:** a small amount of storage in your browser so the site works, such as remembering your cookie choice.
- **Analytics (optional):** Google Analytics and, where enabled, Microsoft Clarity, which show us how the site is used: visits, clicks, scrolling and anonymous heatmaps or session replays. With this on, your browser also keeps a short history of the pages you've viewed on this site and where you first came from; if you send us a form, that history is included with it so we understand what you were interested in. Visitors in the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland start with analytics off until they choose.
- **Marketing (optional, off until you accept):** Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads tags, which let us show our ads to people who have visited the site and measure whether those ads lead to enquiries.

We don't sell your information.

## How we use it

- To reply to your enquiry and, if we work together, to run the project
- To send the newsletter, if you signed up (every email has an unsubscribe link)
- To understand which pages and services people are interested in, and improve the site
- If you accept marketing cookies, to show you our ads on other websites and apps and measure how they perform
- To keep the site secure and working

## Who else handles it

We use a small number of services to run the site, and they process data on our behalf: **Vercel** (website hosting), **FormSubmit** (delivering form submissions to our inbox), **Google** (email, Google Analytics and, if you accept marketing cookies, Google Ads), **Microsoft** (Clarity, if you accept analytics cookies) and **Meta** (the Meta pixel if you accept marketing cookies, and WhatsApp if you choose to message us there). Form submissions are shared within our team so the right person can reply.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to respond and for our business records, and delete them sooner if you ask. Analytics data is kept for a limited period set in our Google Analytics account.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent you've given (for example, to the newsletter or analytics cookies). Email [connect@kloutkrew.in](mailto:connect@kloutkrew.in) and we'll respond as quickly as we can. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may be able to complain to the data protection authority where you live; in India, that's the Data Protection Board of India.

## Children

This site is meant for businesses and isn't directed at children.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date below.
