Video sells products that photos can't explain. Here are formats that work for D2C brands.

| # | Format | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem → product → result | Ads |
| 2 | Unboxing | Social, product pages |
| 3 | How to use in 15 seconds | Product pages, Reels |
| 4 | Ingredient or material close-ups | Trust and quality |
| 5 | Before and after | Beauty, cleaning, home |
| 6 | Founder story | Brand building |
| 7 | Customer UGC | Ads and social proof |
| 8 | Comparison with alternatives | Consideration |
| 9 | Behind the scenes | Authenticity |
| 10 | Stop-motion or animated product | Attention and fun |
| 11 | AI-generated world around the product | Launches and campaigns |
| 12 | FAQ answers | Reducing hesitation |

## Tips

- Hook in the first two seconds
- Subtitles; most viewers watch muted
- Vertical first for social
- Make cut-downs for each platform
