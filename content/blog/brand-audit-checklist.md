A **brand audit** shows where your brand is strong, where it's inconsistent and what to fix first. Use this checklist and score each line from 0 to 2.

## Identity

- Logo used correctly everywhere (no stretched or outdated versions)
- One defined colour palette in use
- Consistent typefaces
- Brand guidelines exist and are shared

## Website

- Loads fast on mobile
- Clearly says what you do and for whom in the first screen
- Matches current branding
- Has clear calls to action and working forms

## Social

- Same name, avatar and bio style on every platform
- Posts recognisably on-brand without the logo
- Regular posting rhythm

## Packaging and print

- Current packaging matches the brand
- Range is easy to navigate
- Reads well as a marketplace thumbnail

## Reputation

- Google reviews and directory profiles up to date
- Consistent name, phone and address everywhere

## Competitors

- You can explain in one sentence how you differ
- You're visually distinct from your three closest competitors

## Scoring

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Missing or inconsistent |
| 1 | Exists but needs work |
| 2 | Strong |

Low scores in identity suggest a [refresh](/blog/signs-you-need-a-rebrand). Low scores in website or reputation are often quicker wins. We run brand and website audits as part of our [branding](/branding-agency) work.
