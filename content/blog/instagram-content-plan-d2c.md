Posting randomly burns time. A simple **Instagram content plan** keeps a D2C brand consistent and on-message.

## Pick four content pillars

| Pillar | Examples |
|---|---|
| Product | Demos, how-tos, ingredients, launches |
| Proof | Reviews, UGC, before and after |
| Brand | Founder story, values, behind the scenes |
| Community | Tips, trends, questions, giveaways |

## A weekly rhythm

| Day | Post |
|---|---|
| Mon | Reel: product how-to |
| Wed | Carousel: education or comparison |
| Fri | Reel: UGC or customer story |
| Daily | Stories: polls, behind the scenes, offers |

Saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks and sales from Instagram, not just likes.
