Most restaurant website visits happen on a phone, minutes before deciding where to eat. A **restaurant website** should answer questions instantly.

## The essentials

| Item | Tip |
|---|---|
| Hours and location | Top of the page, with a map link |
| Menu | HTML, not PDF, with prices |
| Book or order | One clear button |
| Photos | Real dishes and the space |
| Contact | Tap-to-call and WhatsApp |
| Events and private dining | If relevant |

## Speed

Large food photos must be compressed. A slow site loses hungry visitors. See [Core Web Vitals](/blog/core-web-vitals-explained).

## Local SEO

Keep your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-guide) updated with photos, menu and hours, and use the same details on your site.
