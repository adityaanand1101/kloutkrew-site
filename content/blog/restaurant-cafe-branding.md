People choose restaurants with their eyes first: on Instagram, on a delivery app, on the street. **Restaurant branding** makes a place feel like somewhere worth going.

## Start with a concept

What's the experience in one line? "A neighbourhood bakery that feels like a friend's kitchen" leads to very different design than "late-night Korean street food".

## The touchpoints

| Touchpoint | Tip |
|---|---|
| Name and logo | Easy to say and search; works on a signboard and an app icon |
| Signage | Readable from across the street, lit at night |
| Menu | Clear hierarchy, prices easy to scan, matches the room |
| Interiors | One "Instagram moment" people want to photograph |
| Delivery packaging | Branded, functional, keeps food in good condition |
| Website | Menu, hours, location, booking or order links |
| Social | Consistent photo style and templates |

## Delivery apps

On Swiggy and Zomato your logo is tiny and your photos do the selling. Invest in consistent food photography and a logo that reads at small sizes.

## Local visibility

Set up a [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-guide) with photos, menu and hours. Most "near me" searches start there.
