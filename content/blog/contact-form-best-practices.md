A broken or annoying form loses business silently. Here's how to get **contact forms** right.

## Keep it short

| Field | Needed? |
|---|---|
| Name | Yes |
| Email or phone | At least one |
| Message | Yes |
| Budget, service, timeline | Optional dropdowns if they help you reply |
| Company size, address | Usually no |

## Make it easy

- Visible labels, not just placeholder text
- Large tap targets on mobile
- Clear error messages next to the field
- A button that says what happens: "Send enquiry"

## After submitting

Show a clear confirmation and say when you'll reply. Send an auto-reply email if possible.

## Spam protection

A hidden honeypot field catches most bots without hurting real users.

## Test and track

Forms break silently when sites change. We once found a client form posting to a page that didn't exist, so enquiries were lost. Test monthly and track submissions in GA4.
