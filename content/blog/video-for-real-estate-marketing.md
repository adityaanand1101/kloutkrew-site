Property is an emotional purchase. Video helps buyers imagine living there before they visit.

## Formats that work

| Format | Purpose | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Project brand film | Story and lifestyle | 60–90 s |
| Walkthrough | Layout and finishes | 2–4 min |
| Location film | Connectivity and neighbourhood | 30–60 s |
| Construction updates | Trust and progress | 30–60 s monthly |
| Testimonial | Social proof from buyers | 30–90 s |
| Ad cut-downs | Meta and YouTube ads | 6–30 s |

## Pre-launch with AI and 3D

Before construction, renders and AI-assisted lifestyle scenes can carry the story. Keep them representative of the real project.
