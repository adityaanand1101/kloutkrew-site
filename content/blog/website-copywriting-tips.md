Design gets attention; words make the decision. Here's how to write **website copy** that works.

## Lead with the customer

Swap "We are a passionate team of creatives" for "Packaging that makes your product the obvious choice on shelf."

## Be specific

| Vague | Specific |
|---|---|
| High-quality service | First concepts within 7 days |
| Fast turnaround | We reply within 24 hours |
| Many happy clients | Named clients and real reviews |

## Structure service pages

1. What the service is and who it's for
2. What's included
3. How the process works
4. Proof: work and testimonials
5. FAQs
6. Call to action

## Use customer language

The words clients use in emails and calls are often the words they search for. Use them in headings and FAQs.

## Calls to action

Say exactly what happens: "Get a quote", "Book a call", "See the work".

## Avoid unverifiable claims

Numbers you can't back up damage trust with both people and AI search tools. See [brand voice](/blog/brand-voice-guide) and our [website service](/website-design-agency).
