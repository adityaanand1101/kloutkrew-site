In a market full of similar towers, **real estate project branding** is often what makes buyers book a site visit.

## Naming

A project name should be easy to say, suggest the lifestyle or location, and be free to use as a domain and social handle. Avoid names so similar to nearby projects that buyers confuse them.

## Identity

The identity should reflect the buyer: premium projects lean on restraint and craft; family projects on warmth and green space. The identity must work on hoardings, brochures, the sales gallery and phone screens.
