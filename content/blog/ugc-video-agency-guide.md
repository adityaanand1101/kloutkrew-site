UGC is one of the most effective ad formats for D2C brands. A **UGC video agency** makes it repeatable.

## What's usually included

| Stage | Agency handles |
|---|---|
| Creator sourcing | Matching creators to your audience |
| Briefing | Messages, hooks, shot lists |
| Production | Product shipping, follow-up, reshoots |
| Editing | Hooks, captions, cut-downs, ad formats |
| Rights | Usage agreements for organic and paid |

## How to evaluate

- Do their videos feel natural, not like scripted ads?
- Can they deliver several hooks per video for testing?
- Do they understand your category and claims?
- Are usage rights clear?
