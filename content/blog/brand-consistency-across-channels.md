As brands grow, more people create more things in more places. Without a system, the brand fragments.

## The system

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Brand guidelines](/blog/brand-guidelines-what-to-include) | Rules for logo, colour, type, imagery, voice |
| Templates | Social, decks, ads, documents, email |
| Asset library | One shared folder of approved logos, fonts and photos |
| Owner | One person approves new formats |
| Review rhythm | Monthly spot checks, yearly audit |

## Channel by channel

- **Website:** the reference point for everything else
- **Social:** templates and a consistent photo style
- **Packaging:** same colours and type as digital. See [brand and packaging together](/blog/brand-and-packaging-design)
- **Marketplaces:** listing images that match your site
- **Ads:** flexible within clear rules
- **Sales materials:** deck and proposal templates

## Allow flexibility

Consistency doesn't mean everything looks identical. Define what's fixed (logo, colours, type) and what can flex (layouts, campaign graphics).
