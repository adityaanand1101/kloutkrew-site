As companies grow from one product to many, a question appears: should each product have its own name and look? That's **brand architecture**.

## The main models

| Model | How it works | Best when |
|---|---|---|
| Branded house | One master brand, descriptive product names | Products share an audience and promise |
| Endorsed brands | Distinct product brands "by" the parent | New audiences, but parent adds trust |
| Sub-brands | Product names with their own identity within the master | Ranges need personality |
| House of brands | Separate brands, parent mostly invisible | Very different audiences or price points |

## Questions to decide

1. Does the new product serve the same customer?
2. Could it hurt the main brand if it fails?
3. Will it be sold in different channels or at a different price?
4. Can you afford to market a separate brand?
