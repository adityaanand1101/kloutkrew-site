AI has made film-quality ads possible on smaller budgets. But a good **AI ad film** still follows a disciplined process.

## 1. Brief

Message, audience, channels, length, mandatory product shots and deadline.

## 2. Script

A tight story with a clear hook in the first two seconds and one main message.

## 3. Storyboard

Frame-by-frame plan, approved before production. This is where most decisions should happen.

## 4. Generation and capture

| Element | Approach |
|---|---|
| Worlds and scenes | AI image and video generation |
| Product | Real photography or accurate 3D renders |
| People | Generated, stock or filmed, depending on the brief |
| Motion and transitions | Generated and edited |

## 5. Edit, sound and colour

Human editors build rhythm and story. Sound design and music make it feel finished. Colour grading keeps shots consistent.

## 6. Versions

Cut-downs for every placement: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5. See [video aspect ratios for social](/blog/video-aspect-ratios-social).

## 7. Review

Check brand accuracy, product correctness and claims before release.
