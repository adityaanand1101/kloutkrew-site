A brand film is often the first piece of content people share about you. Here's how to plan one in Delhi NCR.

## Define the goal

Launch? Recruitment? Investor story? Product explainer? The goal decides length, tone and where it runs.

## Choose the approach

| Approach | Best when |
|---|---|
| Shoot | Real people, places and product handling matter |
| AI-led | Big visual ideas, tight budget or timeline |
| Hybrid | Product shots filmed, worlds generated |

## Plan the logistics

- Locations and permissions if shooting
- Talent and wardrobe
- Product samples ready and perfect
- Approvals from the people who decide

## Timeline

1. Brief and script
2. Storyboard
3. Shoot or generation
4. Edit, sound, colour
5. Versions for every channel
