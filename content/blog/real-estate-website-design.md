A property buyer will look at a project's website several times before booking a site visit, and their family and banker will often look too. A good **real estate website** makes the project easy to understand, builds trust in the developer and makes it easy to enquire. A poor one wastes your ad budget.

## What is a real estate project website?

A real estate project website is a page or small site dedicated to one development. It explains **what is being built, where, for whom and when**, shows the homes or offices in detail, and turns interest into enquiries and site visits. Developers usually pair it with a main brand website that lists every project.

## The sections buyers look for

| Section | What buyers want to know | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Project name, location, configuration (e.g. 3 and 4 BHK) | Put the configuration and location in the first screen |
| Gallery and video | What it will look and feel like | Mix renders, construction progress and a walkthrough |
| Floor plans | Layouts, sizes and orientation | Make plans zoomable and downloadable |
| Location | Commute times, schools, hospitals, metro | Use a map with distances to key landmarks |
| Amenities | Clubhouse, green areas, security | Show them, don't just list them |
| Specifications | Flooring, fittings, structure | Clear tables beat long paragraphs |
| Price and payment plan | Budget fit | "Price on request" is fine, but explain the payment plan |
| Developer and approvals | Can I trust them? | Track record, delivered projects and RERA registration where applicable |
| Enquiry | How do I find out more? | Short form, call and WhatsApp buttons visible on mobile |

## Features that generate enquiries

1. **Sticky mobile contact bar** with call, WhatsApp and enquiry buttons.
2. **Short forms.** Name, phone and preferred configuration is usually enough.
3. **Brochure download** in exchange for contact details.
4. **Site-visit booking** with date options.
5. **Tracked conversions**, so every form, call and WhatsApp click is recorded in analytics and your ad platforms.

## Speed and ads

Most property leads now come from Meta and Google ads opened on a phone. Every second of loading costs you visitors you've already paid for. Compress renders into modern image formats, keep videos short and lazy-loaded, and test pages with Google Lighthouse. A project page that loads in under three seconds on mobile will usually convert better than a more elaborate one that loads in eight.

## Trust signals

Buyers are making one of the biggest purchases of their lives. Show the developer's delivered projects, construction progress photos with dates, approvals and RERA details where they apply, bank tie-ups, and real testimonials from past buyers.

## Brand website vs project microsite

| | Brand website | Project microsite |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Developer reputation and all projects | Sell one project |
| Audience | Buyers, investors, partners, media | Buyers from ads and channel partners |
| Lifespan | Long term | Launch to sell-out |
| Best for | Trust, SEO, investor relations | Ad landing pages, conversion |

## Checklist

- Configuration and location in the first screen
- Zoomable floor plans
- Location map with travel times
- Construction updates with dates
- Developer track record and approvals
- Call and WhatsApp buttons on mobile
- Short enquiry form, tracked in analytics
- Mobile load time under three seconds
