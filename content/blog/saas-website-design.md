A SaaS website has one job: help the right visitors understand the product fast enough to try it.

## Homepage messaging

Answer in the first screen: what it does, for whom, the main outcome, and how to start. Show the product, not abstract illustrations.

## Key pages

| Page | Tip |
|---|---|
| Features | Organise by outcome, not by menu items |
| Use cases | One page per audience or industry |
| Pricing | Clear tiers, FAQ under the table |
| Customers | Logos, quotes and short case studies |
| Integrations | Searchable list |
| Docs and resources | Good for SEO and AI search |

## Product visuals

Short looping clips or interactive tours beat static screenshots. Keep them light so pages stay fast.

## Sign-up flow

Fewer fields, clear next steps and a quick first success inside the product. See [app onboarding UX](/blog/app-onboarding-ux).
