Good feedback makes revisions faster and the final work better.

## Principles

| Principle | Example |
|---|---|
| Refer to the brief | "Does this feel premium enough for our price?" |
| Describe the problem | "The flavour is hard to spot at small size." |
| Be specific | "The headline font feels too playful." |
| Prioritise | "Must change" vs "nice to have" |
| Consolidate | One document, one decision-maker |

## Helpful vs unhelpful

| Unhelpful | Helpful |
|---|---|
| "I don't like it." | "It feels cold; our brand is warm and family-focused." |
| "Make it pop." | "The product name needs more contrast on the shelf." |
| "Can you try 10 more options?" | "Can we explore a version with a bolder colour?" |

## Review in context

Judge designs on a phone, as a printed proof or in a shelf mockup, not only in a slide.
