AI can place your product in a sunlit kitchen, a beach or a studio set in minutes. Used carefully, **AI product photography** saves time and budget.

## Where AI helps

| Use | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Lifestyle backgrounds | No location shoots |
| Seasonal campaigns | Fast festive variations |
| Ad testing | Many creative options |
| Concepts before shoots | Better briefs |

## Where to be careful

- **Product accuracy:** shape, colour, label text must be exact
- **Hands and people:** check details closely
- **Claims:** don't imply features the product doesn't have
- **Marketplace rules:** main images must show the real product

## A reliable workflow

1. Shoot or render the product accurately
2. Generate backgrounds and scenes
3. Composite and retouch by hand
4. Check against the real product
