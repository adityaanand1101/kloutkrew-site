Great brand films feel effortless because the structure underneath is tight.

## The five-part structure

| Part | Job | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Stop the scroll | An unexpected image or question |
| Tension | Show a problem or desire | The rush of a working morning |
| Turn | Introduce the brand | The product changes the moment |
| Proof | Make it believable | Detail, demo, real reaction |
| Close | Brand and one action | Logo, line, call to action |

## By length

- **15 seconds:** hook, turn, close
- **30 seconds:** all five, briefly
- **60 seconds:** room for story and emotion

## Writing tips

- Write for picture first; words support images
- Read it aloud with a timer
- Plan subtitles and on-screen text
- Leave space for music and sound
