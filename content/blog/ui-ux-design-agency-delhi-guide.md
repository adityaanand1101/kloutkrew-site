A good **UI/UX design agency** helps you build a product people understand and enjoy using. Here's what to expect and how to choose one.

## What a project usually includes

| Phase | Output |
|---|---|
| Discovery | Goals, users, competitors, analytics review |
| UX | User flows, information architecture, wireframes |
| UI | High-fidelity screens in your brand |
| Prototype | Clickable flows for testing |
| Handover | Design system, specs and assets for developers |

## What to check in a portfolio

- Real shipped products, not just concepts
- Problem and outcome explained, not only screenshots
- Mobile and web experience
- Design systems and documentation

## How to brief

Share your users, key tasks, business goals, technical constraints and timeline. See [wireframe vs mockup vs prototype](/blog/wireframe-vs-mockup-vs-prototype) and the [UX audit checklist](/blog/ux-audit-checklist).
