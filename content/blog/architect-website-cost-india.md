Architecture firms are judged on design, so their websites need more care than a standard business site. Prospective clients, developers and competition juries will look at your site and form an opinion about your work within seconds. This guide explains what an architect's website costs in India in 2026, what drives the price, and how to get a quote you can compare.

## Typical market ranges in India

Published 2026 pricing guides give a wide spread:

| Type | Typical range quoted |
|---|---|
| Basic template site (freelancer) | ₹5,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Template customisation by an agency | ₹15,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Custom design | ₹1,00,000 – ₹5,00,000+ |

Sources: [Hostinger India](https://www.hostinger.com/in/tutorials/website-cost), [DEV Community pricing guide](https://dev.to/secuodsoft/website-development-cost-in-india-2026-complete-pricing-guide-1ld4).

An architecture firm's portfolio site usually falls in the custom design range, because the presentation of projects is the whole point of the site.

## Why architecture sites cost more than typical business sites

- **Image-heavy pages.** Large, high-quality photographs and drawings need careful handling so they look sharp but still load quickly on phones.
- **Project storytelling.** Each project page needs a structure: the brief, the site, the concept, drawings, materials, photographs and credits.
- **Filtering and organisation.** Visitors want to browse by typology (residential, commercial, hospitality), location or year.
- **Content preparation.** Selecting, editing and writing up projects often takes longer than the design itself.
- **Brand expectations.** Clients expect the site to reflect the firm's design sensibility, so off-the-shelf templates rarely satisfy.

## What drives the cost

- Number of projects to set up and write
- Custom layouts vs a template
- Photography and drawings preparation
- A content management system so your team can add projects
- Animations and interactive features such as before-and-after sliders or 3D walkthroughs
- Multiple languages or office locations
- SEO setup and content

## What you should get at each level

| Level | Includes |
|---|---|
| Starter | Template, 5–8 pages, a few projects |
| Professional | Custom design, project CMS, image optimisation, SEO basics |
| Premium | Bespoke design, film, interactive features, content and SEO plan |

## Costs beyond the build

- **Domain and hosting:** usually a modest yearly cost, higher on platforms like Webflow with a paid site plan.
- **Photography:** professional architectural photography is often the best investment you can make for the site. Budget per project.
- **Copywriting:** if nobody in the studio has time to write project descriptions, plan for a writer.
- **Maintenance:** adding new projects, updating the team page and keeping software current.

## Choosing a platform

- **Webflow** suits most architecture firms: strong design control, a visual editor for adding projects, and fast hosting.
- **WordPress** works if you need specific plugins or already have an in-house developer.
- **Framer** is quick for smaller studios that want a simple, elegant portfolio.

See [Webflow vs Framer vs WordPress](/blog/webflow-vs-framer-vs-wordpress) for the trade-offs.

## How to get an accurate quote

Before you ask for quotes, prepare:

1. **The number of projects** you want online at launch, and roughly how many you add each year
2. **Your page list:** home, projects, studio, people, process, awards, press, careers, contact
3. **Three sites you admire** and what you like about each
4. **Who will update the site** after launch
5. **Your deadline,** for example a competition entry or a new office opening

Comparing quotes is much easier when each studio is pricing the same scope.

## Common mistakes

- Uploading full-resolution photographs that take ten seconds to load on a phone
- Hiding the contact details or the enquiry form
- Listing every project ever built instead of curating the best
- No text on project pages, which gives search engines nothing to rank
