Architecture firms are judged on design, so their websites tend to need more care than a standard business site. Here's how pricing works.

## Typical market ranges in India

Published 2026 pricing guides give a wide spread:

| Type | Typical range quoted |
|---|---|
| Basic template site (freelancer) | ₹5,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Template customisation by an agency | ₹15,000 – ₹50,000 |
| Custom design | ₹1,00,000 – ₹5,00,000+ |

Sources: [Hostinger India](https://www.hostinger.com/in/tutorials/website-cost), [DEV Community pricing guide](https://dev.to/secuodsoft/website-development-cost-in-india-2026-complete-pricing-guide-1ld4).

An architecture firm's portfolio site usually falls in the custom design range.

## What drives the cost

- Number of projects to set up and write
- Custom layouts vs a template
- Photography and drawings preparation
- CMS so your team can add projects
- Animations and interactive features
- Multiple languages or locations
- SEO setup and content

## What you should get

| Level | Includes |
|---|---|
| Starter | Template, 5–8 pages, a few projects |
| Professional | Custom design, project CMS, image optimisation, SEO basics |
| Premium | Bespoke design, film, interactive features, content and SEO plan |
