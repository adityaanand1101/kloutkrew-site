Clients hire interior designers they trust with their homes and money. An **interior designer website** builds that trust before the first call.

## Must-have pages

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Your best project and a clear line on what you do and where |
| Portfolio | Filter by residential, commercial, room type |
| Project pages | Before and after, brief, budget range if you share it, photos |
| Services | Full design, consultation, turnkey execution |
| Process | Steps from first meeting to handover |
| About | Founder, team, philosophy |
| Contact | Short form, WhatsApp, studio location |

## Photography matters most

Invest in professional photos of finished projects. Large, fast-loading images in modern formats keep quality high without slowing the site.

## Local search

Set up a [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-guide) and create location pages if you work in several cities.
