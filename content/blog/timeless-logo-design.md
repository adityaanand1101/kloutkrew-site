A logo is a long-term investment. Trends can make it feel fresh today and dated in two years. Here's how to build one that lasts.

## Principles that last

- **Simplicity.** Fewer elements are easier to remember and reproduce.
- **Distinctive silhouette.** You should recognise it as a solid shape.
- **One clear idea.** Not five symbols combined.
- **Good proportions.** Careful spacing and optical balance.
- **Works in one colour.** For stamps, embroidery, foil and fax-quality prints.

## Trends to treat with care

| Trend | Risk |
|---|---|
| Heavy gradients and 3D effects | Hard to print, dates quickly |
| Ultra-thin line logos | Disappear at small sizes |
| Copying a famous minimalist style | Looks generic |
| Very trendy display fonts | Feel dated fast |

## Let the system carry trends

Keep the logo simple and let campaign graphics, motion and social content follow trends. That way the brand feels current without a full redesign.

## Test for longevity

1. Show it at 16 pixels and on a billboard.
2. Print it in black only.
3. Put it next to logos from ten years ago and see if it would have fitted then too.
