Most viewers decide in the first seconds whether to keep watching. Here are **hook formulas** to adapt.

## Hook formulas

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Mistake | "You're applying sunscreen wrong." |
| Number | "3 packaging mistakes that cost sales." |
| Before / after | Show the result first |
| Question | "Why does your website feel slow?" |
| Bold claim (true) | "This label took 12 versions." |
| POV | "POV: your box arrives looking like this." |
| Myth | "Minimal packaging isn't always premium." |
| Curiosity | "We tested 5 hooks. One won by miles." |
| Direct callout | "D2C founders, watch this before you print." |
| Process | "From sketch to shelf in 30 seconds." |

## Visual hooks

- Movement in the first frame
- A surprising close-up
- Text on screen in the first second

## Keep the promise

The hook must match the content, or viewers leave and the algorithm notices.
