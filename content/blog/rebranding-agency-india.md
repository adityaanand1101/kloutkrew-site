A rebrand touches everything. A good **rebranding agency** manages both the creative and the change.

## The typical process

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| Audit | Review current brand, competitors and customer perception. See [brand audit checklist](/blog/brand-audit-checklist) |
| Strategy | Positioning, architecture, what to keep |
| Design | New identity, tested on real touchpoints |
| Guidelines | Rules and templates |
| Rollout plan | Priorities, order and owners |
| Launch | Announcement and coordinated updates |

## Prepare your team

- Agree why you're rebranding and what success looks like
- Name one decision-maker
- List every touchpoint that will need updating

## Protect what works

Keep recognisable assets where possible, and protect search visibility with redirects if names or domains change. See [how to rebrand without losing customers](/blog/rebrand-without-losing-customers).
