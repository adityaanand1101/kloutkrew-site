More people now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI features for recommendations: "best branding agency in Delhi", "which packaging studio works with D2C brands", "how much does a logo cost in India". These tools answer with a short list and a few sources. **AI search visibility**, sometimes called generative engine optimisation (GEO), is about making your business easy for those systems to find, understand and recommend. It's best thought of as an extension of SEO, not a replacement for it.

## How AI answers are put together

Most AI assistants draw on two things: what they learned in training, and pages they retrieve from the web when you ask a question. When they retrieve, they lean on search indexes and on sources that state facts clearly. That's why the basics of SEO still matter: if a page isn't indexed, it can't be retrieved.

## What helps AI recommend you

| Signal | What to do |
|---|---|
| Clear facts | State what you do, where, for whom, on your site |
| Structured data | Organization, Service, FAQ schema |
| Answer-style content | FAQs and guides that answer real questions |
| Consistency | Same name and details everywhere |
| Third-party mentions | Directories, reviews, press, partner sites |
| Reviews | Genuine reviews on Google and Clutch |
| Freshness | Updated content and dates |

## Write a plain fact block

Somewhere prominent, often the about page and the footer, state the essentials in plain sentences: your business name, what you do, where you're based, who you work with, and how to contact you. AI systems summarise; give them a clean summary to work from rather than making them interpret marketing slogans.

## Answer real questions

Collect the questions customers ask before buying, such as cost, timelines, process and ownership, and answer each clearly on your site. Put short answers near the top of the page and detail below. Dedicated FAQ pages and FAQ sections on service pages both help.

## Use structured data

Schema markup tells machines what a page is about: your organisation, services, FAQs and articles. It doesn't guarantee anything, but it removes ambiguity.

## Be consistent everywhere

Use exactly the same business name, address, phone, email and description on your website, Google Business Profile, social profiles and directories. Conflicting details make it harder for any system to be confident about you.

## Earn mentions from other sites

AI answers often cite third-party pages such as directories, review platforms, press and "best of" lists. Getting listed on relevant directories, publishing useful original research and contributing to industry publications all increase the chance you're mentioned.

## Practical steps

1. Write a plain fact block about your business
2. Add FAQs to service pages
3. Get listed on relevant directories
4. Earn mentions through useful content and partnerships
5. Track a set of prompts monthly

For step 5, write down ten questions a customer might ask an AI assistant, ask them in two or three tools once a month, and record whether you appear and which sources are cited. It's a simple way to see progress.

## What doesn't work

Keyword stuffing, fake reviews and hidden text. AI systems and search engines penalise manipulation. Mass-produced thin articles don't help either: they dilute your site and give AI tools nothing worth citing.

## How long it takes

Like SEO, AI visibility builds over months. New websites and young domains take longer because they have few mentions elsewhere. Focus on genuinely useful content and real reputation, and measure trends rather than week-to-week changes.
