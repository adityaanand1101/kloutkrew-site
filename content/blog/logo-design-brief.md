A **logo design brief** is a short document that tells a designer what your logo needs to do. A good brief leads to better first concepts and fewer revision rounds. A vague one ("make it modern and clean") leads to guesswork.

## The questions your brief should answer

1. **What does your business do, in one sentence?**
2. **Who are your customers?** Age, location, what they care about.
3. **Who are your competitors?** Name three and say what you think of their branding.
4. **What personality should the logo have?** Pick three adjectives, such as bold, warm, precise.
5. **Where will it appear?** Packaging, app icons, signage, social avatars, embroidery.
6. **Any must-haves or no-gos?** Colours you must avoid, symbols tied to your culture or category.
7. **What's the timeline and who decides?**

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Asking for a logo that explains everything.** A logo identifies; it doesn't describe every service.
- **Designing by committee.** Agree one final decision-maker before concepts arrive.
- **Ignoring small sizes.** Your logo must work as a 32-pixel favicon and an Instagram avatar.
- **Judging the logo alone.** Ask to see it on a pack, a website header and a social post.
