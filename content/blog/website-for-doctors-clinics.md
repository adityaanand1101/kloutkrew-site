Patients choose clinics they trust and can book easily. A **clinic website** should feel calm, clear and credible.

## Key sections

| Section | What to include |
|---|---|
| Services | One page per major treatment, explained simply |
| Doctors | Photos, qualifications, experience, languages |
| Booking | Online booking, call and WhatsApp buttons |
| Location and timings | Map, parking, directions |
| Patient info | What to bring, insurance, preparation |
| FAQs | Common questions about treatments and costs |

## Design principles

- Calm colours and generous spacing
- Large, readable text for all ages
- Fast, mobile-first pages
- [Accessibility](/blog/website-accessibility-basics) built in

## Local search

Most patients search "near me". Keep your [Google Business Profile](/blog/google-business-profile-guide) complete and request reviews politely.

*Check the advertising and patient-privacy rules that apply to your practice.*
