Good UGC feels natural but is carefully briefed. Use this template.

| Section | Include |
|---|---|
| Product | What it is, price, who it's for |
| Key messages | Two or three points to mention |
| Hooks to try | Three opening lines |
| Must-show shots | Unboxing, product in use, result, close-up of label |
| Style | Natural light, vertical, real setting |
| Don'ts | Medical claims, competitor names, filters that change colours |
| Deliverables | Number of videos, lengths, raw clips |
| Usage rights | Organic and/or paid, duration, platforms |
| Deadline and payment | Clear dates and terms |

## Tips

- Send the product early so creators actually use it
- Ask for several hooks per video to test
- Request raw clips for editing into ads
