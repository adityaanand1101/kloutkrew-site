Many brands design a logo with one person and packaging with another. The result is often a pack that doesn't quite fit the brand. Designing **brand and packaging together** avoids that.

## Packaging is where most people meet your brand

For product brands, the pack is the most-seen brand asset, on shelves, in apps and in unboxing videos. The identity should be designed with packaging in mind from day one.

## Benefits of one team

| Benefit | Why |
|---|---|
| Consistency | Logo, colours and type designed for real packs |
| Speed | No re-briefing between agencies |
| Cost | Shared research and concepts |
| Better logos | Tested on pack before being finalised |
| Easier ranges | System thinking from the start |

## What a combined project includes

1. Positioning and naming if needed
2. Logo and identity system
3. Packaging structure and artwork
4. Mockups, listing images and launch content
5. Brand guidelines covering packaging rules
