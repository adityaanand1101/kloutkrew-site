Many users decide whether an app is worth keeping within their first session. **Onboarding UX** should get them to a first win quickly.

## Define the first win

What's the moment users "get it"? Their first project created, first report seen, first order placed. Design everything to reach it fast.

## Principles

| Principle | Example |
|---|---|
| Ask less upfront | Collect details later, when needed |
| Show, don't tell | Pre-filled sample data or templates |
| Progressive set-up | A short checklist with progress |
| Helpful empty states | Explain what goes here and offer one action |
| Timely nudges | Reminders tied to unfinished steps |

## Mistakes

- Long carousels before users can do anything
- Asking for permissions before explaining why
- Forcing account details users don't need yet

## Measure and iterate

Track the first-win rate and week-one return, then improve the steps that lose people.
