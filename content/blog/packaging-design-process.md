Packaging design is part creative and part technical. Here's how a well-run **packaging design process** works.

## 1. Brief

Product, price point, customer, sales channels, competitors, launch date and budget. Include mandatory text and any dielines your printer has.

## 2. Research and shelf audit

Look at the category in store and online. Which colours dominate? What do shoppers see first? Where is there space to stand out?

## 3. Concepts

Two or three directions, each shown on a 3D mockup, on a shelf and at marketplace thumbnail size.

## 4. Refinement

Develop the chosen route: hierarchy, claims, illustrations or photography, and legibility of mandatory information.

## 5. Range artwork

Extend the design across sizes, flavours and variants so each is easy to tell apart.

## 6. Print files

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Dieline match | Folds and cuts land correctly |
| Bleed and safe zones | No white edges or cut-off text |
| CMYK / Pantone | Colours print as intended |
| Finishes marked | Foil, emboss, spot UV on separate layers |
| Barcode test | Scans at retail |

## 7. Proof and launch assets

Approve a printed proof, then produce mockups, listing images and social assets.
