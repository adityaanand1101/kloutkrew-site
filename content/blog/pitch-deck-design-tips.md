Investors skim decks quickly. Good design helps them get your story fast.

## A common structure

Problem · Solution · Product · Market · Business model · Traction · Competition · Team · Financials · Ask

## Design principles

| Principle | In practice |
|---|---|
| One idea per slide | A headline that states the takeaway |
| Readable | Large type, high contrast |
| Real visuals | Product screenshots, photos |
| Simple charts | Highlight the one number that matters |
| On-brand | Your colours, type and logo |
| Consistent | Same layout grid throughout |

## Headlines that say something

Not "Market" but "A ₹X crore market growing every year" (with your sourced figures).

## Two versions

A presenting version with fewer words, and a sending version that stands alone.
