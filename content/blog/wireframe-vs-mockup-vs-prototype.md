These three terms come up in every website and app project. Here's what each one means.

## At a glance

| | Wireframe | Mockup | Prototype |
|---|---|---|---|
| Detail | Low | High | High, interactive |
| Purpose | Structure and flow | Visual design | Testing interactions |
| Looks like | Grey boxes and text | Final screens | Clickable screens |
| Change cost | Very low | Medium | Medium |

## Why the order matters

Agreeing structure in wireframes before visual design avoids expensive redesigns later. Prototypes then let you test flows with real users before development.

## Do you need all three?

- **Small marketing site:** wireframes and mockups
- **Complex site or app:** all three, plus testing
