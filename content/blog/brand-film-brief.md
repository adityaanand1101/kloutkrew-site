A clear brief makes a better film. Use this template.

| Section | Your answer |
|---|---|
| Goal | What should viewers think, feel or do? |
| Audience | Who are they, where will they watch? |
| Key message | One sentence |
| Tone | Three adjectives |
| Channels and formats | YouTube, Reels, website, ads, events |
| Length | Hero film and cut-downs |
| Mandatory elements | Logo, product shots, claims, legal lines |
| References | Films you like and why |
| Budget range | Helps choose AI, shoot or hybrid |
| Timeline | Key dates and approvers |

## Tips

- One message beats five
- Name who approves at each stage
- Share brand guidelines and product samples early
