User-generated content converts 4x better than brand-produced content. That number comes from multiple studies and it holds across industries. Here is why UGC is the most powerful content strategy most brands are not using to its full potential.

## Why UGC Works

People trust people more than brands. When a real customer posts a photo of themselves using your product, it carries an implicit promise that "this works." That social proof is something no amount of brand-produced content can replicate.

UGC also solves the authenticity problem. In an era where consumers are increasingly skeptical of polished marketing, the imperfect, unfiltered nature of UGC feels genuine — and genuine drives conversion.

## How to Build a UGC Strategy

Start by making it easy for customers to create and share content. Simple prompts like "show us how you use this" or a branded hashtag can unlock a flood of authentic material. Then amplify the best UGC by featuring it on your social channels, website, and even paid ads.

The key metric to watch is not volume — it is **conversion rate**. Track how UGC-driven landing pages compare to brand-produced ones. You will almost always see a significant lift.

## Legal and Ethical Considerations

Always get explicit permission before reposting customer content. A simple DM or written consent is sufficient. Credit the creator prominently, and respect their right to remove content if they change their mind.
