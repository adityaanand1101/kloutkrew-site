A **packaging redesign** can lift sales, but changing too much can lose customers who shop by colour and shape.

## Signs you need one

- You look like everyone else in your category
- New variants don't fit the current design
- Your packs don't read on apps and marketplaces
- Quality or price has moved up, but packaging hasn't
- Customer feedback mentions confusing packs

## Keep what customers recognise

List your **distinctive assets**: colour, logo placement, shape, a character or pattern. Keep the strongest one or two.

## Refresh vs redesign

| Scope | What changes |
|---|---|
| Refresh | Typography, hierarchy, finishes, photography |
| Redesign | New system, structure or identity |

## Roll it out

1. Test new designs in mockups next to competitors
2. Update listing images on the same day new stock ships
3. Tell customers: "New look, same product"
4. Phase old stock out rather than wasting it
