Both can do excellent work. The right choice depends on what you need, how fast, and how much managing you want to do.

## Side by side

| | Freelancer | Branding agency / studio |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Lower | Higher |
| Range | One or two specialisms | Strategy, design, web, film, content |
| Capacity | One person's time | A team, can run in parallel |
| Management | You manage the project | Project management included |
| Continuity | Depends on one person | Team cover for holidays and illness |
| Best for | Focused tasks | Launches, rebrands, multi-channel work |

## Questions to ask yourself

1. How many pieces do I need: just a logo, or packaging, website and content too?
2. Do I have time to brief and coordinate several freelancers?
3. How important is strategy before design?
4. What happens if the person I hire becomes unavailable?
