Delhi NCR has hundreds of web design companies, from solo freelancers to large agencies. Here's how to choose well.

## Check their work properly

- Open their sites on your phone; are they fast and easy to use?
- Run a few through PageSpeed Insights
- Ask which ones are live client sites, not concepts

## Questions to ask

1. Which platform do you recommend for us, and why?
2. Who writes the content?
3. What SEO setup is included?
4. How many revision rounds?
5. Will we be able to edit the site ourselves?
6. Who owns the design files and domain?
7. What happens after launch?

## Compare quotes fairly

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Custom or template | Big cost and quality difference |
| Number of pages | Scope clarity |
| Content and photos | Often excluded |
| SEO and analytics | Should be standard |
| Hosting and maintenance | Ongoing costs |

## Red flags

- No live examples
- Prices given before understanding your needs
- They keep ownership of your domain or files
- No mention of mobile speed or SEO
