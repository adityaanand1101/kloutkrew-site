On Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, your product appears as a square thumbnail among dozens of others. **Packaging for quick commerce** must work at that size.

## The thumbnail test

Shrink your pack image to about 100 pixels wide and place it next to competitors. Can you tell what it is, which brand, and which variant?

## Design rules for small screens

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| One focal point | Busy packs turn to noise when small |
| Own a colour | Colour is recognised before text |
| Big, simple brand mark | Readable at thumbnail size |
| Variant colour-coding | Shoppers find their flavour fast |
| Front-facing shot | Apps crop angled photos badly |

## Listing images

Beyond the main pack shot, add images that answer questions: size in hand, key benefits, ingredients, how to use. See our [Amazon listing images guide](/blog/amazon-listing-images-guide).

## Don't forget the shelf

Quick-commerce packs are still delivered and kept in kitchens and bathrooms, so the physical experience still matters.
