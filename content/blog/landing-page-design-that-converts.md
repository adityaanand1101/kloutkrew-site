A good landing page does one job. Here's how to design one that converts.

## The structure

1. **Hero:** what it is, who it's for, the main benefit and one button
2. **Proof:** client logos, reviews, numbers you can back up
3. **How it works:** three simple steps
4. **Details:** features, what's included, pricing or "from" price
5. **Objections:** FAQs answering doubts
6. **Final call to action:** repeat the button and form

## Design rules

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| One goal, one button style | Choice reduces conversions |
| Match the ad's message | Visitors should feel they're in the right place |
| Short forms | Every field costs enquiries |
| Real photos over stock | Builds trust |
| Fast on mobile | Ads traffic is mostly mobile |

## Test

Change one thing at a time: headline, hero image, form length. Track conversions in GA4.
