Your homepage is a signpost. In a few seconds it should tell visitors they're in the right place and where to go next.

## Above the fold

- **Headline:** what you do and for whom
- **Subline:** the main benefit or difference
- **Primary button:** the most important next step
- **Visual:** your product or best work

## Below the fold

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Proof | Logos, reviews, results |
| Services or products | Short overview with links |
| Work or examples | Show, don't tell |
| How it works | Three or four steps |
| Testimonials | Real names and roles |
| FAQ | Answer common doubts |
| Final call to action | Repeat the main action |

## Mistakes to avoid

- Clever slogans that don't say what you do
- Sliders that rotate messages nobody reads
- Heavy animations that delay the main message
- Unverifiable claims and stats
