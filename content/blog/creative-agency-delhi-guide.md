"Creative agency" covers a lot of ground. Here's how the main types differ.

## Agency types

| Type | Focus | Typical outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Branding agency | Identity and strategy | Naming, logo, guidelines, packaging |
| Creative agency | Ideas and content | Campaigns, design, films, social |
| Ad agency | Advertising campaigns | TV, print, digital ads, media plans |
| Digital agency | Online channels | Websites, SEO, paid media, social |
| Production house | Making films | Shoots, editing, post-production |

## Which do you need?

- **Launching a new brand:** branding first, then creative and digital
- **Growing sales online:** digital and creative
- **Big awareness campaigns:** creative or ad agency
- **Need everything consistent:** an integrated studio
