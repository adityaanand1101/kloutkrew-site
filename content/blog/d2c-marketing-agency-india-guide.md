D2C growth depends on a steady supply of good creative, smart media buying and retention. The right agency covers the gaps in your team.

## What to look for

| Capability | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Creative production | Ads fatigue quickly |
| Performance media | Budget efficiency |
| Brand consistency | Long-term trust |
| Retention (email, WhatsApp) | Repeat purchases |
| Analytics | Honest reporting |

## Questions to ask

1. How many new creatives will we get each month?
2. How do you decide what to test?
3. What do your reports include?
4. Who works on our account day to day?
5. What do you need from us?

## Red flags

- Promising guaranteed numbers
- Reporting only likes and reach
- No access to your own ad accounts
