LinkedIn rewards founders who share useful, specific experience. Here's a simple system.

## Post types

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| Lesson | "What we learned from redesigning 10 packs" |
| Story | A client problem and how it was solved |
| Opinion | A clear view on an industry debate |
| Behind the scenes | Process, sketches, drafts |
| Framework | A carousel with steps or a checklist |
| Milestone | Launches and wins, with context |

## Writing tips

- First line is the hook; make it specific
- Short paragraphs, plain words
- One idea per post
- End with a question or a clear next step

## Turn posts into leads

Link your profile to a clear offer and a landing page. Reply to every comment; conversations lead to calls.
