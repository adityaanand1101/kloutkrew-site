For a D2C brand, the box arriving at the door is the first time a customer touches the brand. A good **unboxing experience** turns that moment into a story worth sharing.

## Layers of the experience

| Layer | Idea |
|---|---|
| Outside | Branded mailer or tape so it's recognised on the doorstep |
| Opening | A message on the inside lid |
| Reveal | Product presented, not buried in filler |
| Extras | Thank-you card, how-to guide, sample |
| After | QR code to reorder, review or follow |

## Budget-friendly ideas

- Print inside the box instead of outside
- Use a one-colour design on kraft board
- Branded stickers and tissue
- A handwritten-style note printed in bulk

## Design for video

Customers film unboxings on phones. Use strong colour inside the lid and avoid glossy surfaces that glare.

## Protect the product

Breakage destroys the experience. Test with real courier journeys.
