Food is bought with the eyes. **Food packaging design** has to make the product look delicious, signal quality and give practical information, all on a small surface.

## Appetite appeal

Show the food at its best: rich photography, honest illustrations or a window onto the real product. Avoid stock images that look like every competitor.

## Flavour and variant systems

| Approach | Example use |
|---|---|
| Colour per flavour | Snacks and beverages |
| Ingredient illustration | Spreads, sauces, teas |
| Pattern per variant | Premium ranges |

## Practical details

- Resealable closures for multi-use packs
- Clear cooking or serving instructions
- Space for batch codes and dates
- Legible mandatory information. See [food label design](/blog/food-label-design-india).

## Online and delivery

On apps, packs appear tiny. Test designs at thumbnail size, and prepare listing images showing serving suggestions and pack size. See [packaging for quick commerce](/blog/packaging-for-quick-commerce).
