India's D2C market is crowded. In beauty, snacks, coffee, supplements and home goods, a shopper scrolling Instagram or Blinkit sees dozens of new brands a week. **D2C branding** is how one of them gets remembered.

## Start with positioning, not a logo

Before any design, answer three questions in one sentence each:

1. **Who is this for?** Be specific: "working women in metro cities who want quick, clean skincare".
2. **What do we promise?** One clear benefit.
3. **Why should they believe us?** An ingredient, a founder story, a process, a price.

These answers decide colours, tone and packaging later.

## What a D2C brand needs

| Asset | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Logo and identity | Recognition across every channel |
| Packaging | The first physical touchpoint and the unboxing video |
| Product imagery | Sells on your site and marketplaces |
| Website | Your own store and data |
| Listing images | Amazon, Flipkart and quick-commerce thumbnails |
| Social templates | Fast, consistent content |
| Ad creatives | Tested weekly to find what sells |

## Design for the small screen

Most first impressions happen on a phone, often as a thumbnail. Choose a bold colour you can own in your category, a logo that reads at small sizes and packaging with one clear focal point. Read our guide to [packaging for quick commerce](/blog/packaging-for-quick-commerce).

## Build a system, not one-offs

Your identity should make every future post, ad and pack faster to produce. That means templates, a photography style and [brand guidelines](/blog/brand-guidelines-what-to-include) from day one.

## Launch content

Plan the first month before launch: reveal posts, founder story, product demos, UGC and a launch film. Our [AI brand films](/ai-brand-films) service helps brands launch with film-quality video without a big shoot budget.
