Early-stage branding should be fast, focused and flexible. Use this checklist.

## Before launch

- Positioning statement. See [how to write one](/blog/brand-positioning-statement)
- Name checked for domains and trademarks. See [brand naming](/blog/brand-naming-for-startups)
- Logo and core identity
- Website with clear messaging
- Pitch deck template. See [pitch deck design tips](/blog/pitch-deck-design-tips)
- Social profiles and templates

## Can wait

| Item | Why it can wait |
|---|---|
| Full brand book | Start with essentials |
| Merchandise | Not critical to launch |
| Large design system | Build as the product grows |
