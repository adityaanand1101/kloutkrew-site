For local searches and map results, your **Google Business Profile** is often the first impression, before anyone sees your website. When someone searches "branding agency near me" or your business name, the profile shows your reviews, photos, hours and contact details in one place. It's free, and for many local businesses it brings more enquiries than the website itself. Here's how to set it up well and keep it working.

## Setup checklist

| Item | Tip |
|---|---|
| Business name | Your real name only, no keyword stuffing |
| Primary category | The closest match to your main service |
| Secondary categories | Other real services |
| Address or service area | Hide the address if you don't receive visitors |
| Phone and website | Same as on your site |
| Hours | Accurate, including holidays |
| Description | Clear, no links or promotional claims |
| Services | List each one separately |
| Photos | Real work, team and workspace |

## Getting verified

Google needs to confirm your business is real before your profile shows fully. Depending on your business, verification may be by video, phone, email or post. Follow the steps in your profile dashboard, and make sure what you show matches your stated address and business details.

## Choose categories carefully

Your **primary category** has the biggest influence on which searches you appear in. Pick the one that most closely describes your main business, then add secondary categories only for services you genuinely offer.

## Write a useful description

Explain what you do, who you serve and where, in plain language. Avoid capital letters, links, offers and keyword lists. Imagine a potential customer reading it on a phone.

## Photos that build trust

- Your logo and a cover image that represents your work
- Real photos of your work, team and workspace
- New photos regularly; active profiles look more trustworthy

Avoid stock photos. They add nothing and can look misleading.

## Keep it active

- Post updates or project photos weekly
- Add new photos monthly
- Answer questions
- Reply to every review

Posts can announce launches, share a recent project or highlight a service. Keep them short with one clear link.

## Reviews

Reviews are among the strongest signals for local search and the first thing many customers read.

- **Ask at the right moment,** such as just after a successful delivery, with a direct review link
- **Make it easy:** send the link by WhatsApp or email
- **Reply to every review,** thanking positive reviewers and responding calmly and helpfully to negative ones

Never buy or fake reviews, or offer rewards in exchange for positive ones. It breaks Google's policies and can get profiles suspended.

## Consistency

Use the same name, phone and website everywhere: your website, social profiles, directories and listings. Inconsistent details confuse both customers and search engines.

## Track what it brings

The profile's performance section shows how people found you, calls, website clicks and direction requests. Check it monthly. If you add a tracking parameter to your website link, you can also see profile visitors in Google Analytics.

## Common mistakes

- Adding keywords or locations to the business name
- Listing a virtual office address where you don't operate
- Ignoring questions and reviews
- Letting hours go out of date during holidays

## Service-area businesses

If you work at clients' locations or remotely rather than receiving visitors, set a service area and hide your street address. You can still appear in local results for the areas you serve.
