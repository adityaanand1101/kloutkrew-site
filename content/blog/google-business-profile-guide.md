For local searches and map results, your **Google Business Profile** is often the first impression. Here's how to set it up well.

## Setup checklist

| Item | Tip |
|---|---|
| Business name | Your real name only, no keyword stuffing |
| Primary category | The closest match to your main service |
| Secondary categories | Other real services |
| Address or service area | Hide the address if you don't receive visitors |
| Phone and website | Same as on your site |
| Hours | Accurate, including holidays |
| Description | Clear, no links or promotional claims |
| Services | List each one separately |
| Photos | Real work, team and workspace |

## Keep it active

- Post updates or project photos weekly
- Add new photos monthly
- Answer questions
- Reply to every review

## Reviews

Ask for reviews with a direct link. Never buy or fake reviews; it can get profiles suspended.
