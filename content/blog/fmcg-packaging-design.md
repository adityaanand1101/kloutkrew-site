FMCG packs live in some of the toughest environments in retail: crowded kirana shelves, modern trade aisles and quick-commerce grids. **FMCG packaging design** is about being seen and chosen in seconds, at scale.

## Three environments, one pack

| Where | What matters |
|---|---|
| Kirana stores | Bold brand block, readable from a distance, hanging strips and sachets |
| Modern trade | Shelf blocking across a range, clear variant coding |
| Apps | Thumbnail legibility, front-facing pack shots |

## Build a range system

FMCG brands add SKUs constantly. Design a system with fixed brand and layout zones and a flexible zone for variant colour, flavour cue and pack size, so new products can be added quickly and consistently.

## Design for production

- Standardise dielines across sizes
- Plan for the print process (flexo, gravure, offset) from the start
- Limit special finishes to where they matter
- Leave clear space for batch coding and barcodes

## Pricing and pack size cues

Price points and pack sizes drive FMCG decisions. Make quantity and value packs instantly clear.
