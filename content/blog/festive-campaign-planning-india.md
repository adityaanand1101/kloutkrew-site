Festivals are peak buying seasons in India, and the most crowded. Planning early is the advantage.

## Timeline

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 8–10 weeks before | Concept, offers, packaging decisions |
| 6 weeks | Creative production, gifting packs ordered |
| 4 weeks | Ads and content ready, website updates built |
| 2 weeks | Teasers and pre-orders |
| Festival week | Peak ads, influencer posts, fast support |
| After | Thank-you messages, review requests |

## Creative ideas

- A brand-specific story, not generic festival imagery
- Limited-edition packaging or sleeves. See [packaging finishes](/blog/packaging-printing-finishes)
- Gift guides and bundles
- Short films and Reels. See [product video ideas](/blog/product-video-ideas-d2c)

## Logistics

Delivery cut-off dates, stock levels and support capacity matter as much as creative.
