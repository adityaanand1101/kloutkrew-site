A strong brief is the most valuable page in any creative project.

## The one-page template

| Section | Question |
|---|---|
| Background | What's the situation? |
| Objective | What must this achieve? (one main goal) |
| Audience | Who exactly are we talking to? |
| Insight | What do they believe, want or fear? |
| Key message | The one thing they should take away |
| Proof | Why should they believe it? |
| Tone | How should it feel? |
| Deliverables | Formats, sizes, channels |
| Mandatories | Logos, legal lines, claims |
| Budget and timeline | Range and key dates |
| Approvals | Who decides |

## Weak vs strong

**Weak:** "Make an awesome launch campaign for our new product."
**Strong:** "Get urban working women aged 25–35 to try our three-step skincare kit in its first month, by showing it fits a ten-minute morning."
