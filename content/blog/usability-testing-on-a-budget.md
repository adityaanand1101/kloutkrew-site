You don't need a lab to test usability. A few short sessions find the biggest problems quickly.

## The method

1. **Pick three to five tasks** that matter, like "Find the price of X and enquire".
2. **Recruit five people** who match your users.
3. **Ask them to think aloud** while they try the tasks.
4. **Don't help.** Note where they hesitate or fail.
5. **Summarise patterns**, not individual opinions.

## Fix and retest

Fix the top issues and run another small round.
