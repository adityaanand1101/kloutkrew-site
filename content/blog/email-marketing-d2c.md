Ads bring customers; email brings them back. Automated flows work while you sleep.

## The essential flows

| Flow | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Welcome | Introduce the brand, first-order nudge |
| Abandoned cart | Remind and reassure |
| Post-purchase | How to use, ask for a review |
| Replenishment | Reminders for consumables |
| Win-back | Re-engage lapsed customers |

## Design tips

- Simple, branded template with live text
- One main message and button per email
- Mobile-first layout
- Real product and UGC images

## Content tips

Educate as much as you sell: how-tos, behind the scenes, customer stories.
