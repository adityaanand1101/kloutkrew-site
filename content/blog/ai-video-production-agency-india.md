India now has a growing number of studios offering AI video. Here's how to tell the strong ones apart.

## What they offer

- Launch and brand films
- Ad films and performance creatives at volume
- Product visualisation and AI product imagery
- Social content: Reels, Shorts, loops
- Remasters and upscaling of old footage

## How to evaluate an agency

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Finished films, not just clips | Shows editing, sound and story skills |
| Consistency across shots | AI often struggles with continuity |
| Product accuracy | Your product must look exactly right |
| Storyboard stage | Avoids surprises |
| Honest limits | They should say when a shoot is better |
| Rights and usage | Clear ownership terms |

## Questions to ask

1. Which parts will be AI and which filmed?
2. How many revision rounds?
3. Which formats and cut-downs are included?
4. How do you keep characters and products consistent?
