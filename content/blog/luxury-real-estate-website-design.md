Luxury buyers judge the project by the experience of the website. **Luxury real estate website design** should feel as considered as the homes.

## Design principles

| Principle | In practice |
|---|---|
| Restraint | Fewer words, more space, limited colours |
| Imagery first | Full-screen renders, photography and film |
| Refined type | Elegant serif or precise sans, carefully spaced |
| Storytelling | Architecture, materials, location, lifestyle |
| Discreet calls to action | "Arrange a private viewing" |

## Content buyers want

- Architect and design story
- Specifications and materials
- Residence types and floor plans
- Amenities and services
- Location and neighbourhood
- Developer credentials

## Speed still matters

Heavy imagery must be optimised. A luxury site that loads slowly feels cheap. See [Core Web Vitals](/blog/core-web-vitals-explained).
