Influencer marketing can build trust faster than ads, if you choose creators carefully.

## Creator tiers

| Tier | Typical strengths |
|---|---|
| Nano | Very engaged niche audiences, affordable |
| Micro | Good balance of reach and relevance |
| Mid / macro | Reach and awareness |
| Celebrity | Broad awareness, high cost |

## Choosing creators

- Audience matches your customer (location, age, interests)
- Real engagement in comments, not just numbers
- Content quality and tone fit your brand
- Past brand work feels authentic

## Working well together

Brief clearly with a [UGC brief](/blog/ugc-brief-template), agree usage rights and disclosure, and give creative freedom.

## Measure

Codes and links for sales, plus content you can reuse in ads.

*Follow current Indian advertising disclosure guidelines for influencer content.*
