The best content plan is one your team can keep up. Here's how to build a **content calendar** that works.

## Plan in three layers

| Layer | Example |
|---|---|
| Monthly theme | "Packaging month" or a product launch |
| Weekly slots | Mon Reel, Wed carousel, Fri UGC |
| Individual posts | Topic, format, caption, asset |

## Template columns

Date · Platform · Pillar · Format · Topic · Hook · Caption · Asset · Owner · Status · Link

## Batch production

Shoot, design and write in batches every two weeks. It's faster and more consistent.

## Leave room for the moment

Keep one slot a week for trends, news and festive moments. See [festive campaign planning](/blog/festive-campaign-planning-india).

## Review monthly

Keep what worked, drop what didn't.
