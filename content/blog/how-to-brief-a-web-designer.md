A clear brief is the cheapest way to get a better website. Here's what to send a web designer before the first call.

## The brief template

| Section | Questions to answer |
|---|---|
| Goal | What should the site achieve? Enquiries, sales, bookings, credibility? |
| Audience | Who visits, from where, on what device? |
| Pages | Which pages do you need? |
| Features | Shop, booking, blog, forms, multi-language, integrations? |
| Content | What copy, photos and videos exist? Who writes the rest? |
| References | Three sites you like and what you like about each |
| Brand | Logo files, colours, fonts, guidelines |
| Platform | Any preference (Webflow, Shopify, WordPress)? Who will edit it? |
| Budget and timeline | A range and a realistic launch date |

## Tips for a better result

- **Rank your goals.** If enquiries matter more than looking impressive, say so.
- **Name your competitors.** Designers can make sure you stand apart.
- **Decide who approves.** One decision-maker keeps projects moving.
- **Plan content early.** Read our [website copywriting tips](/blog/website-copywriting-tips).
