Webflow has become a popular choice for startups, SaaS companies and design-led brands that want a custom website without the maintenance burden of a traditional CMS. A **Webflow agency in India** can design and build sites to an international standard for both Indian and overseas clients. This guide explains why brands choose Webflow, when it isn't the right fit, what a good Webflow project includes, and how to pick an agency.

## What Webflow is

Webflow is a visual website builder and hosting platform. Designers lay out pages visually, while Webflow generates the HTML, CSS and JavaScript behind them. It includes a content management system (CMS) for blogs, case studies and other repeatable content, and an editor where your team can update text and images without touching the design.

## Why brands choose Webflow

- **Design freedom** without template limits
- **Visual CMS** your marketing team can edit
- **Managed, secure hosting** with no plugins to update
- **Built-in SEO controls** for titles, descriptions, redirects and sitemaps
- **Interactions and animations** without heavy custom code

For marketing sites, where design quality and the ability to publish quickly matter most, that combination is hard to beat.

## When Webflow isn't the best fit

| Need | Better option |
|---|---|
| Large online store | Shopify |
| Complex web app | Custom build |
| Very specific plugin ecosystem | WordPress |

Webflow has e-commerce features, but stores with large catalogues, complex shipping rules or many apps are usually better on Shopify. Read [Webflow vs Framer vs WordPress](/blog/webflow-vs-framer-vs-wordpress) for the full comparison.

## What a good Webflow project includes

1. **Discovery:** goals, audience, page list and content plan
2. **Wireframes:** page structure before visual design
3. **Visual design** in Figma, including mobile layouts
4. **Build** in Webflow with a clean class system so future pages are easy to add
5. **CMS setup** for blogs, case studies, team or locations
6. **SEO setup:** titles, descriptions, redirects from your old site, schema and sitemap
7. **Speed optimisation:** compressed images, lazy loading and lean scripts
8. **Training and handover** so your team can edit confidently

Ask for each of these to be listed in the proposal.

## What to look for in a Webflow agency

1. Live Webflow sites you can test on your phone
2. Clean CMS setup and handover training
3. Attention to speed: check their sites in PageSpeed Insights
4. SEO setup included: redirects, schema, sitemap
5. Design quality matching your brand ambition

Also ask who owns the Webflow project and hosting account. Ideally the site sits in your own Webflow workspace, with the agency invited as collaborators.

## Costs to plan for

Beyond design and build, budget for Webflow's own site plan for hosting, which is billed yearly or monthly by Webflow, plus any paid fonts, integrations or form tools. If you'll publish often, plan for a CMS plan rather than a basic one.

## Migrating from WordPress or another platform

Moving platforms is a chance to tidy up, but it carries SEO risk. Make a list of every URL on the old site and set up redirects to matching new pages, carry over page titles and descriptions where they work, and check Search Console for errors after launch.
