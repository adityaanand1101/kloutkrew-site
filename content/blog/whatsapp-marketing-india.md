For many Indian customers, WhatsApp is the most natural way to talk to a business.

## Where WhatsApp helps

| Use | Example |
|---|---|
| Enquiries | Button on website and Instagram |
| Catalogue | Products with prices in WhatsApp Business |
| Order updates | Confirmations and delivery messages |
| Support | Quick answers and returns |
| Ads | Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns |
| Re-engagement | Opted-in broadcasts for launches |

## Be respectful

Only message people who opted in. Send fewer, more useful messages. Make it easy to stop.

## Set up for speed

Quick replies, labels and simple automation help small teams respond fast.

## Track it

Use tagged links and note which chats became sales.
