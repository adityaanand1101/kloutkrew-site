Finishes add touch and shine to packaging. Used well, they make a pack feel premium; overused, they look busy.

## Common finishes

| Finish | What it does | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Matte lamination | Smooth, non-reflective | Modern, understated packs |
| Gloss lamination | Shiny, colours pop | Vibrant food and toy packs |
| Soft-touch | Velvety feel | Premium beauty and gifting |
| Spot UV | Glossy highlights on selected areas | Logos, patterns |
| Foil stamping | Metallic shine | Luxury cues, festive packs |
| Embossing / debossing | Raised or pressed areas | Logos, tactile details |
| Window cut | Shows the product | Food, toys |

## Rules of thumb

- Pick **one hero finish** per pack
- Make sure finishes survive shipping and handling
- Consider recyclability; some laminations complicate it
- Always see a physical sample

## Specifying for print

Designers prepare a separate layer or file for each finish, aligned to the [dieline](/blog/what-is-a-dieline). Approve a proof before the full run.
