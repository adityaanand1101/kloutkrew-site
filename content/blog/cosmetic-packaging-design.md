Beauty is one of the most crowded D2C categories in India. **Cosmetic packaging** has to look premium on a shelf, in a flat-lay photo and in an unboxing video.

## What matters most

- **A recognisable system.** Customers should spot your brand across serums, creams and cleansers.
- **Clear product naming.** Benefit and key ingredient, not just a clever name.
- **Photogenic materials.** Frosted glass, matte finishes and clean labels photograph well.
- **Usability.** Pumps, droppers and tubes that work and look good after use.

## Designing a range

| Element | Keep fixed | Vary |
|---|---|---|
| Layout | Yes | |
| Typography | Yes | |
| Colour | Base palette | Accent per product |
| Graphics | Logo placement | Ingredient illustration |
