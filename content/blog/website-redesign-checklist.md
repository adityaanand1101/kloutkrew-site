A redesign should improve your site, not reset your traffic. Use this **website redesign checklist**.

## Before you start

- Export a list of every current URL (from your sitemap or a crawler)
- Note your top pages by traffic and enquiries in Analytics and Search Console
- Record current rankings for key keywords
- Save current titles, descriptions and structured data

## During the redesign

- Keep URLs the same where you can
- Map every changed URL to its new address
- Keep or improve content on top-performing pages
- Set titles, meta descriptions and H1s for every page
- Check speed with Lighthouse. See [Core Web Vitals explained](/blog/core-web-vitals-explained)

## Launch day

| Task | Done? |
|---|---|
| 301/308 redirects live for every changed URL | |

## After launch

- Check Search Console coverage and 404 reports weekly for a month
- Compare traffic and rankings with your baseline
- Fix broken links
