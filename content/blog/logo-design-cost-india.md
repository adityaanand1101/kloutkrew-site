"How much does a logo cost?" has no single answer in India. Here's what the market looks like in 2026 and what you get at each level.

## Typical ranges

| Option | Typical range | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Logo makers and beginner designers | ₹0 – ₹1,000 | Templates, little originality |
| Logo templates | ₹500 – ₹6,000 | Pre-made, not unique |
| Design contests | ₹2,000 – ₹10,000 | Many quick options, little strategy |
| Basic professional logo | ₹5,000 – ₹20,000 | Original mark, limited revisions |
| Agency brand identity | ₹1,00,000+ for larger businesses | Strategy, full system, guidelines |

Sources: [Makers Design on Medium](https://medium.com/makersdesign/logo-design-cost-in-india-96e0fb926d3), [Internshala hiring guide](https://internshala.com/blog/employer-cost-to-hire-graphic-designer/), [Foduu](https://www.foduu.com/blog/factors-that-affect-logo-design-pricing-in-india). Globally, [ManyPixels](https://www.manypixels.co/blog/brand-design/logo-design-cost-guide) puts most small-business logos at $300–$2,000.

## What drives the price

- Research and strategy before design
- Number of concepts and revision rounds
- Designer or studio experience
- Deliverables: file formats, variations, guidelines
- Logo alone vs a full brand identity

## What a professional logo should include

- Original design you own
- Primary, secondary and icon versions
- Colour, black and white versions
- Vector files (SVG, PDF, EPS) and PNGs. See [logo file formats](/blog/logo-file-formats-explained)
- Basic usage rules
