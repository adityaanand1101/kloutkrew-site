AI has changed ad film budgets in India. Films that once needed a crew, locations and several shoot days can now be produced with AI-generated visuals, directed and finished by a small team. But "AI film" covers everything from a quick social clip to a polished brand film, and prices vary just as widely. This guide explains what the market looks like in 2026 and what drives the cost.

## The market in 2026

- One industry write-up puts a traditional 30-second digital ad film in India at roughly [₹2 lakh to ₹20 lakh](https://chasingillusionsstudio.blogspot.com/2026/05/why-brands-use-ai-ad-films-india.html), depending on scale and talent.
- A Hyderabad production house quotes [₹5–7 lakh for a 30-second brand or ad film without star cast](https://auromediahouse.com/?p=9414), and notes AI tools cost ₹5,000–25,000 a month each but don't cover direction or brand alignment.
- [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/?p=100348) reported AI cutting a 60-second brand film from ₹10–25 lakh to around ₹50,000–75,000 for one platform, with timelines dropping sharply.

These are examples, not price lists. Your cost depends on scope.

## What you're paying for

The AI tools themselves are a small part of the cost. Most of the budget goes into the same craft as any film:

- **Concept and script:** an idea that fits your brand and the platform it will run on
- **Storyboard:** every shot planned before generation starts
- **Direction:** generating, selecting and refining shots until they're consistent in style, lighting and character
- **Product accuracy:** making sure your actual product and packaging look right on screen
- **Editing, sound and music:** pacing, voiceover, licensed music and sound design
- **Versions:** cutdowns and aspect ratios for each platform

## What drives the cost

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Length and number of cut-downs | More versions, more editing |
| Realism and complexity | Photoreal people and products take more iteration |
| Real product footage | Adds a shoot day or 3D work |
| Music and voiceover | Licensing and talent |
| Revisions | Each round adds time |
| Usage | Broadcast and long-term use may cost more |

## AI vs traditional: where each makes sense

**AI-led production works well for:**

- Launch and concept films where the world can be imagined rather than filmed
- Product visualisation in settings that would be expensive to build or travel to
- Social ads that need many variations quickly
- Pitch films and mood films for investors or retailers

**A traditional shoot is still better for:**

- Close-ups of real people using your product, especially skin, hair and food
- Testimonials and founder stories
- Anything where viewers must trust that what they see is real

Many of the best results combine the two: a short product shoot for accuracy, with AI for environments and scale. See [AI video vs a traditional shoot](/blog/ai-video-vs-traditional-shoot).

## Hidden costs and risks to plan for

- **Revisions after generation.** Changing the concept late means regenerating shots. Approve the storyboard before production starts.
- **Product drift.** AI tools can alter logos and labels. Budget for compositing your real packaging into shots.
- **Music licensing.** Free tracks are fine for organic posts; paid ads and broadcast need properly licensed music.
- **Platform rules.** Some platforms ask advertisers to disclose AI-generated content. Check before you launch.

## Budgeting tips

- Decide the hero film and the cut-downs upfront
- Approve the storyboard before production
- Combine with a small product shoot for accuracy
- Plan a set of short ad variations alongside the main film; they're cheap to produce once the look is built
- Ask for a breakdown by stage so you can compare quotes fairly

## Typical timeline

A short AI-led film usually takes two to four weeks from brief to final files, depending on length, revisions and how much real product footage is needed. Traditional shoots typically take longer because of pre-production and scheduling.
