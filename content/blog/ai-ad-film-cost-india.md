AI has changed ad film budgets in India. Here's what the market looks like and what drives cost.

## The market in 2026

- One industry write-up puts a traditional 30-second digital ad film in India at roughly [₹2 lakh to ₹20 lakh](https://chasingillusionsstudio.blogspot.com/2026/05/why-brands-use-ai-ad-films-india.html), depending on scale and talent.
- A Hyderabad production house quotes [₹5–7 lakh for a 30-second brand or ad film without star cast](https://auromediahouse.com/?p=9414), and notes AI tools cost ₹5,000–25,000 a month each but don't cover direction or brand alignment.
- [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/?p=100348) reported AI cutting a 60-second brand film from ₹10–25 lakh to around ₹50,000–75,000 for one platform, with timelines dropping sharply.

These are examples, not price lists. Your cost depends on scope.

## What drives the cost

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| Length and number of cut-downs | More versions, more editing |
| Realism and complexity | Photoreal people and products take more iteration |
| Real product footage | Adds a shoot day or 3D work |
| Music and voiceover | Licensing and talent |
| Revisions | Each round adds time |
| Usage | Broadcast and long-term use may cost more |

## Budgeting tips

- Decide the hero film and the cut-downs upfront
- Approve the storyboard before production
- Combine with a small product shoot for accuracy
