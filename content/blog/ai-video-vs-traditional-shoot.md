Brands now have a real choice between generating footage and filming it. Here's how to decide.

## Side by side

| | AI video | Traditional shoot |
|---|---|---|
| Speed | Days to weeks | Weeks with pre-production |
| Cost | Lower for big visuals | Higher: crew, locations, talent |
| Imagined worlds | Excellent | Expensive |
| Real product handling | Limited | Excellent |
| Real people and testimonials | Not authentic | Authentic |
| Iteration | Easy to try variations | Reshoots are costly |

## Choose AI when

- Visualising concepts, worlds or moods
- Making many ad variations to test
- Budget or time is tight

## Choose a shoot when

- Trust depends on real people and real use
- Product texture and handling must be exact

## Hybrid is often best
