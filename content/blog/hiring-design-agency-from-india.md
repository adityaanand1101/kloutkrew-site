More brands abroad now work with design studios in India. Done well, it combines high-quality work with good value and a smooth process.

## Why brands choose Indian studios

- Strong design and development talent
- Competitive pricing compared with many Western markets
- English as the working language
- India Standard Time (UTC+5:30) overlaps the Gulf working day and much of Europe's

## How remote projects run

| Stage | How it works |
|---|---|
| Kickoff | Video call and written brief |
| Concepts | Shared files you can comment on |
| Updates | Written summaries at every milestone |
| Approvals | Named decision-makers, clear deadlines |
| Handover | All source files and guidelines |

## How to choose

1. Check live, launched work in your category
2. Read reviews on Clutch and Google
3. Ask how they handle communication and time zones
4. Agree scope, revisions, payments and ownership in writing
