Delhi NCR has hundreds of branding agencies, from solo designers in Lajpat Nagar to large teams in Gurugram's office towers. Most have attractive portfolios, which makes them hard to tell apart. This guide gives you a simple process to shortlist, compare and choose the right one for your brand.

## Step 1: Define what you need

Logo only, full identity, packaging, website, launch content? The scope decides which agencies fit. Write down:

- **What you're launching or changing,** and why now
- **Who your customers are** and where they'll see the brand first: a shelf, Instagram, a marketplace or a sales meeting
- **Your deadline,** such as a product launch, trade show or fundraising round
- **Your budget range.** Agencies can tailor scope to it, but only if they know it

## Step 2: Shortlist on relevant work

| Look for | Why |
|---|---|
| Work in your category | Understands your customers and shelf |
| Case studies with reasoning | Strategy, not just pretty pictures |
| Consistent quality | Not one hero project |
| Real launched work | Printed packs, live websites |

Aim for three to five agencies. More than that and comparing proposals becomes a project in itself.

## Step 3: Check reputation

Read Google and Clutch reviews, ask for two references from recent clients, and look at the brands they've worked with today, not five years ago. If a portfolio piece looks familiar, check the client's current branding; agencies sometimes show work that was never used.

## Step 4: Meet the people who'll do the work

The person who pitches isn't always the person who designs. Ask who will lead your project day to day, how many projects they're running at once, and whether you'll speak to the designers directly.

## Step 5: Compare proposals fairly

Compare process, number of concepts, revisions, deliverables, timeline and who works on your project. A proposal that's half the price may include one concept and no guidelines; one that's twice the price may include packaging and launch templates. Line them up item by item.

| Compare | Questions to ask |
|---|---|
| Process | How do you research before designing? |
| Concepts | How many directions will we see, and how are they presented? |
| Revisions | How many rounds are included? What happens after that? |
| Deliverables | Which files, versions and guidelines do we receive? |
| Ownership | Do we own the final work and source files on payment? |
| Timeline | When do we see first concepts? When are final files delivered? |

## Red flags

- Logo concepts before any questions
- Unclear ownership of files
- Vague timelines
- Unverifiable claims and stats
- A portfolio of only mockups, with no launched work
- Pressure to sign quickly with a "this week only" discount

## Local or remote?

A Delhi-based agency makes in-person workshops, factory visits, product shoots and print checks easy. But location matters less than fit: many Delhi brands work happily with studios in other cities, and many Delhi studios work with brands abroad. If you choose a local agency, use that advantage: meet for the kickoff and review packaging proofs together.

## What a good branding project feels like

You should understand why each design decision was made, see concepts on real applications rather than a white page, and finish with a system your team can use without calling the agency for every social post. If the agency explains its thinking clearly in the first meeting, that's a good sign it will do so throughout.
