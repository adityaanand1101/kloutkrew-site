Delhi NCR has hundreds of branding agencies. Here's how to choose the right one for your brand.

## Step 1: Define what you need

Logo only, full identity, packaging, website, launch content? The scope decides which agencies fit.

## Step 2: Shortlist on relevant work

| Look for | Why |
|---|---|
| Work in your category | Understands your customers and shelf |
| Case studies with reasoning | Strategy, not just pretty pictures |
| Consistent quality | Not one hero project |
| Real launched work | Printed packs, live websites |

## Step 3: Check reputation

Read Google and Clutch reviews and ask for references.

## Step 4: Compare proposals fairly

Compare process, number of concepts, revisions, deliverables, timeline and who works on your project.

## Red flags

- Logo concepts before any questions
- Unclear ownership of files
- Vague timelines
- Unverifiable claims and stats
