Customers notice excess packaging, especially in unboxing videos. **Sustainable packaging** doesn't have to mean higher cost; often it starts with using less.

## Reduce first

- Right-size boxes to cut empty space and filler
- Remove unnecessary inner layers and inserts
- Print fewer colours or use one-colour designs

## Choose materials carefully

| Option | Watch out for |
|---|---|
| Kraft paper and board | Plastic laminations reduce recyclability |
| Mono-material plastics | Easier to recycle than mixed layers |
| Glass | Heavy to ship |
| Compostable films | Need suitable composting facilities |

## Design for reuse and refill

Refill pouches, reusable jars and boxes designed to be kept can reduce waste and build loyalty.

## Claim honestly

Use specific, verifiable statements. Vague green claims damage trust.

## Make it look intentional

Sustainable packs can look premium: strong typography, one ink colour and a natural texture often look more confident than busy designs.
