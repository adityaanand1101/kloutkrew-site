Shopify makes it easy to start selling. **Shopify store design** is what makes visitors buy.

## Homepage

- One clear hero: what you sell and why it's different
- Best-sellers near the top
- Trust signals: reviews, press, guarantees, delivery info

## Collection pages

Clean product grids, useful filters and consistent product photography.

## Product pages

| Element | Tip |
|---|---|
| Photos | Pack shot, in use, close-up, scale |
| Title and price | Clear, with any offer visible |
| Benefits | Three short bullets above the fold |
| Variants | Easy to select on mobile |
| Delivery and returns | Stated near the button |
| Reviews | With photos where possible |
| Sticky add to cart | On mobile |

## Speed

Every extra app and image slows the store. Read [Core Web Vitals explained](/blog/core-web-vitals-explained).

## Mobile first

Most D2C traffic is mobile. Design and test on phones first. See [mobile-first design](/blog/mobile-first-design).
