When someone in Delhi NCR searches "branding agency near me" or "cafe in Hauz Khas", local SEO decides who appears.

## The foundations

| Factor | What to do |
|---|---|
| Google Business Profile | Complete, verified, active. See our [setup guide](/blog/google-business-profile-guide) |
| Reviews | Steady flow of genuine reviews with replies |
| Consistency | Same name, phone and website everywhere |
| Location pages | Useful pages for areas you serve |
| Citations | Relevant directories and industry listings |
| Local content | Posts about local projects and questions |

## Delhi NCR specifics

Delhi NCR covers several cities. Mention the areas you actually serve (Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Faridabad, Ghaziabad) naturally on your site and profile.

## Measure

Track profile views, calls, direction requests and website clicks in your Business Profile insights.
