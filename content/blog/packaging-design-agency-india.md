Packaging is the one piece of design every customer touches. In India's crowded D2C, food, beverage and beauty categories, it often decides whether someone picks your product or the one next to it, on a shelf or a phone screen. This guide explains what a **packaging design agency** does, how to choose one, and what a good process looks like.

## What is a packaging design agency?

A packaging design agency designs the outside of your product so it sells: the **structure** (box, pouch, jar, bottle), the **graphics** (brand mark, colours, illustration, photography), the **information layout** (claims, ingredients, mandatory text) and the **production files** your printer needs. Good agencies also think about where the product is sold, who buys it and what competitors look like.

## Why packaging matters more in India now

Three shifts have raised the stakes for Indian brands:

- **Quick commerce.** On Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, shoppers choose from small thumbnails in seconds.
- **Marketplace competition.** On Amazon and Flipkart, your pack image sits in a grid of near-identical products.
- **D2C unboxing.** For brands selling on their own website, the box is the first physical moment with the customer, and it's what gets filmed and shared.

## What to look for in a packaging design agency

| What to check | Why it matters | What good looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Category experience | Food, cosmetics and supplements have different rules and shelf codes | Packs in your category or a close one |
| Print knowledge | Beautiful files that don't print well cost you a full print run | Talks about dielines, bleed, CMYK, finishes and proofs |
| Thumbnail thinking | Most sales now start on a small screen | Shows designs at marketplace thumbnail size |
| Range architecture | Variants must be easy to tell apart but look like one family | A system, not one-off labels |
| Brand fit | Packaging should match your website, social and ads | Can extend or create your brand identity |
| Clear process | You should know what you'll see and when | First concepts within a week or two, defined revision rounds |

## Questions to ask before you hire

1. **Can I see packaging you've designed that was actually printed and sold?** Mockups alone don't show print quality.
2. **How many concepts will I see, and how many rounds of revisions are included?**
3. **Will you design to my printer's dielines and check the files before print?**
4. **Do you supply mockups and listing images for Amazon, Flipkart and my website?**
5. **Who owns the final files?** You should receive every source file.

## Red flags

- A single concept presented as the only option.
- No mention of print specifications, dielines or proofs.
- Designs only ever shown as flat artwork, never on a shelf or at thumbnail size.
- Prices quoted before anyone asks where the product is sold or how many variants you have.

## What a good packaging process looks like

1. **Brief and shelf audit.** The agency studies your category, competitors and sales channels.
2. **Concepts.** Two or three directions shown on 3D mockups and at thumbnail size.
3. **Refinement.** One route is developed across sizes and variants.
4. **Artwork and print files.** Files built to the printer's dielines, with finishes and colours specified.
5. **Mockups and listing images.** Assets for your website, marketplaces and social launch.

## Packaging, brand and website together

Packaging works best when it's part of a system. If your logo, colours and website were designed separately, the pack often ends up looking like a different company. Briefing one team for [brand identity](/branding-agency), [packaging](/packaging-design-agency) and [website](/website-design-agency) keeps everything consistent and is usually faster.
