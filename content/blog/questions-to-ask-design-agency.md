The right questions save months of frustration. Ask these before you sign.

## About their work

1. Can you show work similar to our project?
2. What was the problem and the result in that project?
3. Can we speak to a past client?

## About process

4. What are the steps from brief to launch?
5. How many concepts will we see?
6. How many revision rounds are included?
7. How do you collect and handle feedback?

## About the team

8. Who will work on our project day to day?
9. Who is our main contact?

## About time and money

10. What's the timeline and what could delay it?
11. What's included and what costs extra?
12. How are payments structured?

## About ownership and after

13. Who owns the final files and source files?
14. Are fonts, images and music licensed for our use?
15. What support do you offer after launch?
