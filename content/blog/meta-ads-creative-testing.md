In paid social, creative is often the biggest lever. A **creative testing system** finds winners faster.

## What to test

| Variable | Examples |
|---|---|
| Angle | Problem, benefit, social proof, comparison |
| Format | UGC video, studio video, static, carousel |
| Hook | First line and first frame |
| Offer | Bundle, free shipping, discount |

## A simple process

1. Make three to six different concepts
2. Run them with equal budgets in one test
3. Judge on cost per result, not engagement
4. Make new variations of the winner
5. Retire fatigued ads and keep testing
