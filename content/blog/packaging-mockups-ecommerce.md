You can launch a product page before a single pack is printed. **Packaging mockups** and 3D renders make it possible.

## Where mockups help

| Use | Benefit |
|---|---|
| Design review | See the pack in 3D before printing |
| Pre-launch pages | Start selling or collecting waitlists early |
| Range images | Every variant, perfectly consistent |
| Ads | Fast creative variations |

## Mockups vs photography vs AI

- **Mockups/renders:** perfect consistency, easy variants
- **Photography:** real texture and trust
- **AI-assisted imagery:** fast lifestyle settings around an accurate product

Read about [AI product photography](/blog/ai-product-photography).

## Accuracy matters

Colours, sizes and finishes must match the real product, or returns and bad reviews follow.
