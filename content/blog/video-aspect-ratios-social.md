One film, many screens. Here are the common formats.

| Ratio | Typical size | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 | 1080 × 1920 | Reels, Shorts, Stories, vertical ads |
| 4:5 | 1080 × 1350 | Instagram and Facebook feed |
| 1:1 | 1080 × 1080 | Feeds, marketplaces |
| 16:9 | 1920 × 1080 | YouTube, websites, presentations |

*Platforms change their recommendations; check current specs before final export.*

## Plan for every ratio

- Frame key action in the centre
- Keep text inside safe zones away from interface overlays
- Shoot or generate at high resolution so crops stay sharp
