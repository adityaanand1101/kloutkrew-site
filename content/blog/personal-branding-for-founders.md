Early on, customers often buy from the founder before they buy from the brand. A focused **founder personal brand** makes that easier.

## Pick your lane

Choose two or three topics you can talk about with real experience: your industry, how you built the product, lessons from hiring. Consistency beats range.

## Set up the basics

| Asset | What good looks like |
|---|---|
| Profile photo | Recent, well lit, face clearly visible |
| LinkedIn headline | What you do and for whom, not just "Founder" |
| About section | Short story, what you're building, how to reach you |
| Banner | Company visual identity or a clear statement |

## A weekly rhythm

- **One original post** with a lesson, story or opinion
- **One visual post** such as a carousel or short video
- **Fifteen minutes a day** commenting thoughtfully on others' posts

## Repurpose

Turn one strong idea into a LinkedIn post, a short video, a newsletter section and a blog article. See our [LinkedIn content guide for founders](/blog/linkedin-content-for-founders).
