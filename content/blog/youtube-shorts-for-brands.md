**YouTube Shorts** combine short-form reach with YouTube's search engine. For brands, that means content can keep being found long after posting.

## Formats that work

- Quick how-tos and tips
- Product demos
- Behind the scenes
- Answers to common customer questions
- Teasers linking to longer videos

## Make them searchable

| Element | Tip |
|---|---|
| Title | Describe the question or topic clearly |
| First seconds | Hook plus on-screen text |
| Description | One or two lines with key terms |
| Linking | Point to a related long video or website |

## Repurpose wisely

Export clean versions without watermarks. Adjust captions for search.
