Launch day goes smoother when every asset is ready. Use this checklist.

## Brand

- Logo suite and identity
- [Brand guidelines](/blog/brand-guidelines-what-to-include)
- Favicon and app icon

## Website

- Homepage, product or service pages, about, contact
- Social share images
- Analytics and conversion tracking
- [SEO basics](/blog/seo-checklist-new-website)

## Social

- Profile images, covers and bios
- Launch posts and templates
- Launch film or Reels

## Sales and PR

- [Pitch deck](/blog/pitch-deck-design-tips)
- One-page company overview
- Press kit with logos and photos
- Email signatures

## Product brands

- Packaging and print files
- [Listing images](/blog/amazon-listing-images-guide)
- Unboxing inserts

## Launch week

- Announcement posts and emails scheduled
- Ads ready to switch on
- Team briefed on messages
